Principal Electrical Design Engineer

Date: 19 Aug 2026

Location:

Bristol, GB, BS35 4GG

Job Function: Technical

Business Unit: Plasma Technology

Are you an experienced Electrical Design Engineer ready to shape the future of engineering delivery? This is a rare opportunity to step into a Principal-level role where your technical expertise, leadership, and vision will directly influence how electrical engineering is delivered across the business.

As our Principal Electrical Design Engineer, you will play a pivotal role in driving innovation, improving standards, and leading the transition to smarter, more efficient ways of working. Beyond design, you’ll be a technical authority and mentor—raising capability across the team and ensuring our solutions are world‑class, compliant, and right first time.

What You’ll Be Doing


Lead the design, development, and implementation of electrical control systems for complex machinery, ensuring compliance with EN60204‑33, EN13849, UL, CSA, and CE marking requirements
Act as a recognised technical leader and subject‑matter expert within the organisation
Champion engineering excellence by ensuring all designs meet internal standards and relevant international regulations
Provide high‑quality technical input for manuals and supporting documentation
Promote and embed professional engineering standards across the department
Collaborate closely with multi‑disciplinary teams to ensure specifications are clear, complete, and optimised
Translate technical requirements into structured, modular, and scalable designs
Support supplier engagement, including subcontractor liaison and site visits when required
Drive the development of design tools, automation, and smarter workflows
Lead continuous improvement initiatives to enhance departmental processes
Support the Electrical Engineering Manager in the day‑to‑day running and strategic development of the department


What You’ll Bring

Qualifications


Degree in Electrical Engineering or a related discipline (postgraduate qualification desirable)


Technical & Professional Skills


Proven experience designing machine control systems
Strong understanding of relevant EN and international standards
Proficiency in AutoCAD Electrical and strong computer literacy (MS Excel, Word, Outlook)
Excellent analytical and problem‑solving skills
Experience in improving processes and developing engineering methodologies
Natural leadership capability with the confidence to guide and mentor others


Personal Attributes


A collaborative team player who actively contributes to shared goals
Passionate about knowledge‑sharing and developing others
Takes ownership of work and continually seeks improvement
Energetic, driven, and comfortable delivering challenging technical solutions to tight deadlines
Commercially aware, understanding the cost and business impact of engineering decisions
A systems thinker who balances detail with the bigger picture
Clear, confident communicator at all levels


Why Join Us?

You’ll join a business where your expertise genuinely matters—where you’ll influence strategy, shape standards, and leave a lasting legacy through the engineers you develop. If you’re looking for a role that combines technical challenge, leadership, and long‑term impact, this is the opportunity you’ve been waiting for.

At Oxford Instruments Plasma Technology, we provide tools for the engineering of micro- and nano-structures, with customers using our process solutions to research and produce materials and semiconductors vital for everything from medical devices and diagnostics to electric cars and quantum computers.

Benefits

In addition to a competitive starting salary, structured career development opportunities, and a good work-life balance, Oxford Instruments offers 25 days annual leave, early finish on Fridays, private healthcare, a share incentive plan, a defined contribution Group Personal Pension Plan, and a flexible benefits package that you can tailor to your own requirements
