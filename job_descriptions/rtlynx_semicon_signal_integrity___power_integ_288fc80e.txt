🚀 WE’RE HIRING | SI/PI ENGINEER – BANGALORE

RTLYNX Semicon India Pvt. Ltd. is looking for an experienced SI/PI Engineer to join our growing semiconductor engineering team in Bangalore.

If you are passionate about high-speed semiconductor systems, Signal Integrity, Power Integrity, SerDes, DDR/LPDDR and advanced SI/PI simulation, this opportunity could be a great fit for you!

🔹 Position: SI/PI Engineer

📍 Location: Bangalore, India

💼 Experience: 7–10 Years

🏢 Industry: Semiconductor / High-Speed Systems

🔹 Key Responsibilities

• Perform end-to-end Signal Integrity (SI) and Power Integrity (PI) analysis across Die, Package, Board and System levels.

• Analyze and optimize high-speed interfaces including SerDes, DDR/LPDDR and other high-speed protocols.

• Perform channel analysis, S-parameter extraction, crosstalk, eye-diagram, jitter and PDN analysis.

• Develop 2D/3D electromagnetic models and perform package and board interconnect analysis.

• Support product development from specification and architecture through design, validation and product release.

• Correlate simulation results with lab measurements and troubleshoot SI/PI issues.

• Collaborate closely with ASIC/SoC, Package, PCB, Hardware, Validation and System Engineering teams.

🔹 Required Skills

✅ 7–10 years of relevant SI/PI engineering experience

✅ Strong understanding of Signal Integrity & Power Integrity fundamentals

✅ Hands-on experience with SerDes, DDR/LPDDR or similar high-speed interfaces

✅ Strong knowledge of Transmission Lines, S-Parameters, Crosstalk, Eye Diagrams, Jitter and PDN

✅ Experience in Package and/or Board SI/PI analysis

✅ Experience with S-parameter extraction, 2D/3D EM modeling and simulation-to-measurement correlation

🔹 EDA / Simulation Tools

Experience with multiple SI/PI tools such as:

Ansys HFSS / SIwave | Cadence PowerSI / SystemSI | Siemens HyperLynx / Advanced Solver | Keysight ADS | HSPICE / Broadband SPICE or equivalent tools.

🔹 Preferred Experience

⭐ Experience with PCIe, XAUI, SGMII, JESD204, 10G-KR or similar high-speed protocols

⭐ Experience with NAND, SSD, DIMM, Memory or high-speed semiconductor products

⭐ Programming/scripting experience in Python, Perl or Tcl

⭐ Strong analytical, debugging and cross-functional communication skills

⚡ IMPORTANT – NOTICE PERIOD

Candidates who are currently serving their notice period OR have a confirmed/clear Last Working Day (LWD) are eligible to apply.

📩 Interested candidates: Please share your updated resume along with your current location, total experience, relevant SI/PI experience, notice period/LWD and contact details.
