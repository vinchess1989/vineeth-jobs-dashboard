About GlobalFoundries 

 GlobalFoundries is a leading full-service semiconductor foundry providing a unique combination of design, development, and fabrication services to some of the world’s most inspired technology companies. With a global manufacturing footprint spanning three continents, GlobalFoundries makes possible the technologies and systems that transform industries and give customers the power to shape their markets. For more information, visit www.gf.com. 

Introduction

Device development for feature rich CMOS technologies (CMOS, NVM, sensors)

Your Role

Design, development and characterization of MOSFETs, non-volatile memory cells or CMOS-based sensors to meet performance and reliability requirements

Design of device test structures and detailed characterization of devices

Working with TCAD engineers to predict device split results

Working with Modeling engineers to produce good device models

Detailed analysis of electrical test data and characterization results

​Required Qualifications

Masters with at least 2 years relevant device experience or PhD  

Familiarity with layout and design rules 

Experience in detailed electrical characterization of devices 

Process Integration experience preferred 

Preferred Qualifications

PhD or Masters in Electrical / Electronics Engineering or equivalent

Field of specialization: Microelectronics

Other Essential Attributes

Strong understanding of device and process physics

Aptitude for optimization and innovation

Ability to work in a multi-disciplinary team in a fast-paced environment

Good communication and interpersonal skills
