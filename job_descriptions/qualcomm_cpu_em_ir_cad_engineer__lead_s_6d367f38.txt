General Summary

As a leading technology innovator, Qualcomm pushes the boundaries of what's possible to enable next-generation experiences and drives digital transformation to help create a smarter, connected future for all. As a Qualcomm Hardware Engineer, you will plan, design, optimize, verify, and test electronic systems, bring-up yield, circuits, mechanical systems, Digital/Analog/RF/optical systems, equipment and packaging, test systems, FPGA, and/or DSP systems that launch cutting-edge, world class products. Qualcomm Hardware Engineers collaborate with cross-functional teams to develop solutions and meet performance requirements.

Minimum Qualifications


Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Electrical/Electronics Engineering, Engineering, or related field and 6+ years of Hardware Engineering or related work experience.



OR

Master's degree in Computer Science, Electrical/Electronics Engineering, Engineering, or related field and 5+ years of Hardware Engineering or related work experience.

OR

PhD in Computer Science, Electrical/Electronics Engineering, Engineering, or related field and 4+ years of Hardware Engineering or related work experience.

Job Description


6 to 15 years of experience in EM/IR and PDN domain is mandatory for this role
Expert user of Synopsys Redhawk SC/ Cadence Voltus power integrity analysis tool and have exposure to PNR/STA and extraction industry standard tools and flow
Support and enhance static IR, dynamic IR, and electromigration flows at the block-level and subsystem levels
Provide guidance and feedback to PDN analysis engineers on EMIR best practices, rules, and methodologies.
Troubleshoot and debug EMIR issues and provide solutions to improve accuracy and efficiency.
Develop, support, enhance scripts that can postprocess EMIR results to dashboard and summarize actionable results for design team
Maintain and release qualified flow releases for large and complex SoCs design implementation and analysis work.
Maximize design productivity with strong/efficient python/tcl/perl scripting skills and customization of tool flows
Qualify EMIR tools with regression management systems and create tests.
Engage with EDA vendors in providing cutting edge out of box solutions for new features and flows.
Collaborate with EDA partners to determine/drive optimal and cutting-edge solutions for effective & efficient CAD solutions
