Signal Integrity / Power Integrity (SI/PI) Engineer




We are looking for an experienced SI/PI Engineer with strong expertise in high-speed semiconductor and system-level Signal Integrity and Power Integrity analysis.

Key Responsibilities




Perform end-to-end SI/PI analysis across Die, Package, Board and System.


Analyze and optimize high-speed SerDes, DDR/LPDDR and other high-speed interfaces.


Perform channel analysis, S-parameter extraction, crosstalk, eye-diagram and PDN analysis.


Develop 2D/3D electromagnetic models and perform package/board interconnect analysis.


Support product development from specification and architecture through design, validation and product release.


Correlate simulation results with lab measurements and troubleshoot SI/PI issues.


Work closely with ASIC/SoC, package, PCB, hardware, validation and system teams.

Required Skills




5–15 years of relevant SI/PI experience.


Strong understanding of Signal Integrity and Power Integrity principles.


Hands-on experience with high-speed interfaces such as SerDes and DDR/LPDDR.


Strong knowledge of transmission lines, S-parameters, crosstalk, eye diagrams, jitter and PDN.


Experience in package and/or board SI/PI analysis.


Experience with S-parameter extraction, 2D/3D EM modeling and simulation-to-measurement correlation.

EDA / Simulation Tools




Hands-on experience with multiple SI/PI tools such as:

Ansys HFSS / SIwave, Cadence PowerSI / SystemSI, Siemens HyperLynx / Advanced Solver, Keysight ADS, HSPICE / Broadband SPICE or equivalent tools.

Preferred Experience




PCIe, XAUI, SGMII, JESD204, 10G-KR or similar high-speed protocols.


NAND, SSD, DIMM, memory or high-speed semiconductor products.


Programming/scripting using Python, Perl or Tcl.


Strong analytical, debugging and cross-functional communication skills.

Experience Level




5–15 years | SI/PI | Semiconductor / High-Speed Systems
