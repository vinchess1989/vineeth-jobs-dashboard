Tiles/Partitions Lead -  Physical Design

  

KEY RESPONSIBILITIES:  

Hands on experience with Block level and Top-level physical implementation.
Good understanding and hands-on experience in lower node technologies. (5nm or below)
Drive the design and implementation of large design blocks from initial concept through to tape-out.
Execute synthesis for high-speed designs and lead the Place and Route processes while meticulously analyzing design results for optimization.
Collaborate closely with cross-functional teams, including RTL/Logic designers, to facilitate seamless integration and problem resolution.
Demonstrate strong problem-solving and work well with cross-functional teams
Good listening, writing, and speaking English
Good communication skills, strong interpersonal skills and flexibility
Dedicated, hardworking and good team player
Familiar with Unix/Linux environment and good at scripts

  

PREFERRED EXPERIENCE:  

Hands on experience in place and route flows including multi-voltage designs
Hands on experience in industry standard Place & Route tools such as Fusion Compiler ,  ICC2 and Innovus
Hands on experience with any of synthesis, STA, or physical verification domains
Strong understanding of low-power flows includes UPF concepts, power-gating, isolation, level-shifter, voltage areas, power-state tables,  electrical checks using FM & VSI
Good control over scripting languages like Python, PERL, TCL and strong debug capabilities

  

ACADEMIC CREDENTIALS:  

Masters or Bachelor’s degree in Electronics Engineering
8-12 years of experience in Physical Design flow and methodology development on advanced nodes such as 5nm & 3nm
Proficiency in one or more scripting languages namely Tcl, Perl, sed/awk
Strong problem-solving skills and analytical thinking
Team player, good work ethic, and excellent communication skill
