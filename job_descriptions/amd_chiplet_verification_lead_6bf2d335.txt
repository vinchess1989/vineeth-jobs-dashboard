SMTS SILICON DESIGN ENGINEER



Lead SOC verification Engineer:

 

AMD is seeking a highly experienced SMTS Design Verification Engineer to lead verification efforts for next-generation chiplet SoC designs. The ideal candidate will possess deep expertise in developing scalable testbenches using SystemVerilog and UVM, demonstrate strong proficiency in formal and assertion-based verification methodologies, and have a understanding of RTL integration.

 

Key Responsibilities:

Lead the development and execution of scalable, reusable SystemVerilog/UVM testbenches for server-class SoC verification.
Drive verification strategy and planning for complex server SoC architectures, including integration of high-speed interfaces and subsystems.
Develop and deploy SystemVerilog Assertions (SVA) for assertion-based verification, ensuring protocol and interface correctness across SoC boundaries.
Apply formal verification techniques, including property checking and formal exploration, to critical SoC blocks.
Collaborate with cross-functional teams (RTL, DFT, validation, firmware, and physical design) to resolve complex design and verification challenges in server SoC environments.
Lead coverage analysis and closure, including functional, code, and formal coverage metrics for SoC domains.
Mentor and guide junior engineers in best practices for scalable testbench architecture, assertion development, formal verification, and RTL integration.
Document verification strategies, methodologies, and results to drive continuous improvement and knowledge sharing across AMD’s server SoC teams.

Required Skills and Experience:

Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Electrical Engineering, Computer Engineering, or related field.
12+ years of experience in design verification, with a focus on scalable SystemVerilog/UVM testbench development for complex SoCs Including GLS & SDF verfications.
Proven experience in developing and deploying SystemVerilog Assertions (SVA) for assertion-based verification.
Strong hands-on experience with formal verification tools and methodologies (e.g., property checking, connectivity checks, formal signoff).
Advanced scripting skills (Python, Perl, etc.) for automation and testbench infrastructure.
Excellent problem-solving, debugging, and communication skills.
Demonstrated leadership in driving verification methodology and mentoring teams.

Preferred Qualifications:

Experience with silicon bring-up and lab validation of server SoCs.
Prior work in large-scale SoC environments and cross-site collaboration.

Experience with AI/ML-based verification tools and emulation/FPGA validation flows
