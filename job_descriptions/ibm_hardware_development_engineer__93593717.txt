Introduction

Join a Team That Builds the Future of Computing

At IBM’s largest European R&D site in Ehningen, Germany, innovation is a team effort. With 1,300 employees and around 200 engineers in our hardware development team, we’re shaping the future of computing—together.

We design the core components behind IBM Z, IBM POWER, and IBM Quantum systems—technologies that power cloud computing, AI, big data, and more. What sets us apart is how we work: across borders, disciplines, and product families. You’ll collaborate with colleagues in the U.S., Israel, and India, using Agile and Design Thinking to drive creativity and continuous learning.

If you're passionate about technology and thrive in a collaborative, forward-thinking environment, we’d love to meet you.

Your Role And Responsibilities

Your Role: Hardware Development Engineer – Signal & Power Integrity

We’re hiring development engineers (f/m/x) to join our Electrical Design Team starting January 1, 2027. As part of IBM’s Electronic Packaging organization, you will design, develop, and integrate innovative hardware solutions spanning server boards, processor modules, and validation hardware for IBM’s flagship IBM Z, POWER, and Quantum platforms.

Working in close collaboration with global development teams, you will take ownership of hardware solutions from concept and architecture through implementation, and validation, driving innovation across the full development lifecycle.

Key Areas of Work


Signal Integrity: Model, analyze & optimize industry-leading high-speed interconnects.
Power Integrity: Electrical modeling of power delivery networks for enterprise-class systems and high-current processors.
Packaging Design: Develop electrical solutions for single- and multi-chip modules using advanced packaging technologies.
Hardware Test: Support hardware bring-up, measurements, and failure analysis.
AI & Engineering Productivity: Explore, develop, and adopt AI-assisted engineering workflows to accelerate design, verification, analysis, and development activities.


Preferred Education

Master's Degree

Required Technical And Professional Expertise

We’re looking for candidates with a strong technical foundation and a collaborative mindset. Ideally, you have:


A Master’s or PhD in Electrical Engineering, Computer Engineering, Computer Science, Physics, or a related field
Knowledge of signal integrity, power integrity, high-speed design, and circuit design
Knowledge of electromagnetic simulation methodologies
Familiarity with PCB technologies and materials
Experience with PCB design, modeling and simulation tools (e.g. Ansys HFSS, CST, Keysight ADS, Cadence, Synopsys, Mentor Graphics, Altium)
Programming experience (preferably Python)
Experience with AI-assisted development tools, coding assistants, or workflow automation is a plus
Proactive, self-driven approach combined with a strong commitment to teamwork
Excellent communication skills in English and German
