As a member of the design team, you will be involved in the specification, design and verification of various subsystems and/or testchips.

Your key responsibilities will include developing the RTL and design intent deliverables (SDC, UPF), integrating IPs, running various design checks and writing part of micro-architecture specification. Additionally, you will work with the verification team to review test plans and help debug design issues and the implementation team to optimise those designs. You will plan your work, provide tracking and report status in order to ensure high-quality and timely delivery.
