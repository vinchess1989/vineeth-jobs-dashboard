About Analog Devices

Analog Devices, Inc. (NASDAQ: ADI) is a global semiconductor leader that bridges the physical and digital worlds to enable breakthroughs at the Intelligent Edge. ADI combines analog, digital, AI, and software technologies into solutions that combat climate change, reliably connect humans and the world, and help drive advancements in automation and robotics, mobility, healthcare, energy and data centers. With revenue of more than $11 billion in FY25, ADI ensures today's innovators stay Ahead of What's Possible. Learn more at www.analog.com and on LinkedIn and X.

Principal Product Development Engineer

About The Role

At Analog Devices, you will be a strategic technical leader driving innovation and technical excellence across our product engineering organization. You will serve as a technical authority for complex engineering challenges, lead breakthrough design methodologies, and collaborate with senior leadership to shape product strategy. Your expertise and vision will influence our future semiconductor technology roadmap and organizational direction.

Key Responsibilities


Drive strategic technical direction for product development
Lead innovation in design methodologies and testing approaches
Serve as a technical authority for complex engineering challenges
Mentor teams and influence organizational direction
Collaborate with senior leadership on product strategy


Must Have Skills


Product Reliability: Industry-recognized expertise in reliability engineering with thought leadership publications
Electronic Design: Distinguished mastery of electronic design principles with strategic organizational impact
Mechanical/Optical Systems: Strategic leadership in complex systems engineering across multiple domains
Digital Signal Processing: Pioneering contributions to DSP implementation with industry-wide recognition
Production Optimization: Thought leadership in production optimization methodologies that define industry standards
Integrated Circuits: Distinguished expertise in IC architecture with contributions to next-generation technologies
Qualification Testing: Strategic influence on qualification standards that shape organizational and industry practices


Preferred Education And Experience


Master's or PhD preferred in Electrical Engineering or related field
12+ years of experience with demonstrated technical leadership
Industry recognition in technical domain
Strategic influence on organizational direction
