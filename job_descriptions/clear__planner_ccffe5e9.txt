Project Planner

Location: Finland, Helsinki

Salary: Up to €75,000 + Rotation Package (Accommodation, Flights & Living Allowance)




Join a Leading International Engineering Contractor

Our client is a globally recognised engineering contractor delivering some of the world's most complex and technically challenging projects across the Data Centre, Life Sciences and Advanced Technology sectors. With a strong reputation for innovation, quality and delivering large-scale mission-critical facilities, they continue to expand across Europe and are seeking an experienced Project Planner to join their team on a major hyperscale data centre project in Finland.




This is an excellent opportunity to work on one of Europe's flagship data centre developments, offering long-term career progression, exposure to cutting-edge projects and the chance to work alongside some of the industry's leading professionals.




The Role

As Project Planner, you will play a key role in the successful delivery of a large-scale hyperscale data centre, developing and maintaining project programmes whilst working closely with project management, commercial and construction teams to ensure milestones are achieved.




Responsibilities

Develop, maintain and update detailed project programmes using Primavera P6.
Produce baseline programmes, lookahead schedules and progress updates.
Monitor project performance against programme and identify potential risks or delays.
Carry out critical path analysis and recommend recovery strategies where required.
Work closely with project managers, engineers, commercial teams and subcontractors to ensure programme alignment.
Prepare weekly and monthly planning reports for senior management and the client.
Monitor progress throughout design, procurement, construction and commissioning phases.
Participate in planning meetings and provide programme support across the project lifecycle.
Assist with change management and programme impact assessments.




Requirements

Previous experience as a Project Planner or Planning Engineer within construction or engineering.
Experience using Primavera P6 is essential.
Experience delivering large-scale projects such as Data Centres, Pharmaceutical, Industrial, Infrastructure or Mission Critical developments.
Strong understanding of project controls, scheduling and critical path analysis.
Excellent communication and stakeholder management skills.
Ability to work collaboratively within multidisciplinary project teams.




What's on Offer

Salary up to €75,000
Rotation package
Flights provided
Accommodation provided
Living allowance
Opportunity to work on a flagship hyperscale data centre project
Excellent long-term career progression with a leading international contractor
Exposure to some of Europe's largest mission-critical projects
