Staff Physical Design Engineer

Location: Bengaluru

Experience: 8–12 Years

Employment: Full-time

Role Overview

We are looking for a Staff Physical Design Engineer with strong expertise in full-chip physical implementation, timing closure, and Innovus. The ideal candidate will own critical aspects of physical design from netlist to GDSII, drive PPA optimization, and lead complex timing/debug challenges across advanced SoC designs.

Key Responsibilities

Own full-chip physical design from floorplanning through placement, CTS, routing, optimization, and signoff.
Drive PPA optimization across performance, power, and area.
Strong hands-on implementation experience using Cadence Innovus.
Perform STA, timing analysis, and timing closure across multiple PVT/MMMC scenarios.
Analyze and resolve setup/hold violations, congestion, IR/EM-related issues, and signal integrity challenges.
Work closely with RTL, synthesis, STA, DFT, and physical verification teams to resolve design issues.
Drive clock-tree synthesis, clock optimization, useful skew, and high-quality clock distribution.
Handle full-chip integration, block-level interfaces, hierarchical implementation, and top-level timing closure.
Develop/debug Tcl/Perl/Python automation for implementation, analysis, and flow improvement.
Contribute to methodology improvements and mentor junior engineers.

Required Qualifications

8–12 years of hands-on experience in Physical Design.
Strong full-chip / top-level SoC physical design experience.
Excellent hands-on expertise with Cadence Innovus.
Strong understanding of STA and timing closure methodologies.
Solid experience with floorplanning, placement, CTS, routing, optimization, and signoff.
Strong knowledge of MMMC, SDC constraints, timing ECOs, and PVT analysis.
Experience debugging complex setup/hold, congestion, clock, and routing issues.
Strong understanding of physical design fundamentals and advanced-node challenges.
Proficiency in Tcl scripting; Python/Perl knowledge is a plus.
Strong problem-solving, ownership, and cross-functional collaboration skills.

Good to Have

Experience with high-performance / low-power SoCs.
Exposure to PrimeTime, Tempus, Voltus, Quantus, or equivalent signoff tools.
Experience with IR/EM, crosstalk/SI, physical verification, and ECO implementation.
Experience driving PD methodologies and flow automation.
Experience leading complex blocks or full-chip PD execution.
