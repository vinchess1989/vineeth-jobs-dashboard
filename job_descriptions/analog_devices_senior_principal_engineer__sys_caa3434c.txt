Senior Principal Engineer, Systems Design/Architecture Engineering

About the Role:

The ADI Industrial Architecture Team is seeking an experienced Senior Principal Engineer, Systems Design/Architecture Engineering with mixed signal and RF CMOS IC design and architecture experience. The candidate will be a key member of an advanced architecture team and drive an industry-leading high-performance Industrial portfolio into new and more highly integrated market segments. The candidate will have the opportunity to develop system level designs incorporating high-performance and high-frequency RF, data converter, SERDES, DSP and PLL subsystems. A demonstrated track record of driving industry-leading performance and innovation, as manifested through patents, peer-reviewed publications and/or industry-leading product leadership and definition, is required. The ideal candidate is a self-motivated individual and with strong technical, analytical and communication skills. The role will involve significant collaboration with customers, partners and internal centers-of-excellence.

Key Responsibilities:


Define, prototype and implement multi-GHz digital communication and instrumentation systems.
Lead the definition of and direct the joint implementation of the HW/SW ecosystem encompassing the new product families and customer systems.
Performs detailed system analysis, including power and cost optimization, dynamic reconfiguration and calibration.
Engage with key customers and standards groups to identify market requirements and trends.


Qualifications:


BSEE required, MSEE preferred.
20+ years of experience in system applications engineering system design/architecture engineering, and design engineering of high-performance RF and mixed-signal IC products and systems.
Expertise in SERDES, RF CMOS, PLL, or data converter design.
Experience with FinFET or fine-line planar analog CMOS process technologies and tradeoffs.
Proficiency in software-defined CMOS communications or radar system design.
Knowledge of digitally-assisted high-speed analog design.
Excellent presentation and writing/communication skills.


Preferred Skills:


Leadership experience in broadband communications subsystem projects.
Strong understanding of multi-GHz digital communications.
Proven track record in defining and architecting high-performance communications products incorporating RF ADCs, DACs, PLLs and SERDES.
Experience in specifying data converters for wideband digital communication systems.
Ability to manage multi-channel synchronization and crosstalk effectively.
