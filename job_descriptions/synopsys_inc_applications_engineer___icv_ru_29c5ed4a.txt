We Are

Synopsys is the leader in engineering solutions from silicon to systems, enabling customers to rapidly innovate AI-powered products. We deliver industry-leading silicon design, IP, simulation and analysis solutions, and design services. We partner closely with our customers across a wide range of industries to maximize their R&D capability and productivity, powering innovation today that ignites the ingenuity of tomorrow.

You Are

You have spent years in the trenches of physical verification, debugging LVS mismatches at 2 a.m. and hunting down obscure DRC violations that block tapeout. The difference between a runset that works and one that causes three weeks of respins is something you understand viscerally, and you are the engineer who catches the edge case before it becomes a customer escalation.

You are fluent in the language of foundries, process nodes, and design rules. When a new technology node drops, you do not wait for perfect documentation. You dig into the specs, write test cases, script your way through qualification, and deliver a working rule deck while others are still scheduling kickoff meetings. Perl, Tcl, and Python are not just tools for you, they are how you turn repetitive manual work into reliable automation.

Mentorship matters to you. You have seen junior engineers struggle with the same problems you solved years ago, and you take the time to walk them through it, not just hand them the answer. When a customer calls with a complex layout issue, you can translate their panic into a structured debug plan and explain the root cause in a way that makes sense to both the designer and the FAE.

At Synopsys, you will work on IC Validator runsets for the most advanced process nodes in the industry, directly with foundries and customers who depend on what you build.

What You'll Be Doing


Develop and validate DRC, LVS, and Fill runsets for IC Validator, targeting advanced semiconductor process nodes from 7nm to 3nm and beyond
Work directly with foundry partners to translate process design kits and manufacturing requirements into production-ready rule decks
Automate runset qualification workflows using Perl, Tcl, and Python, reducing manual verification cycles and improving consistency across releases
Debug complex layout verification failures, including LVS topology mismatches, DRC false positives, and DFM rule interactions that block customer tapeouts
Interface with customers and field application engineers to gather requirements, reproduce issues, and deliver technical solutions that keep their projects on track
Mentor junior engineers on PV best practices, IC Validator capabilities, and effective debugging techniques
Track competitive EDA tools like Calibre and Pegasus, understanding their feature sets and how IC Validator differentiates in the market


The Impact You Will Have


Enable first-pass silicon success for customers by delivering robust, accurate PV runsets that catch real issues without false noise
Accelerate IC Validator adoption at top foundries and design houses, directly contributing to Synopsys revenue and market share in the PV space
Reduce customer tapeout delays by providing fast, expert resolution to verification blockers that would otherwise cost weeks of schedule slip
Build automation that scales across multiple process nodes and customer engagements, multiplying your impact beyond individual projects
Shape the future roadmap of IC Validator by feeding real-world customer pain points and competitive insights back to the product development team
Strengthen foundry partnerships through technical excellence and responsiveness, making Synopsys the go-to EDA partner for next-generation process nodes
Grow the technical capability of the team by sharing knowledge, creating reusable scripts, and documenting solutions that become team standards


What You'll Need


B.Tech, M.Tech, or MS in Electronics, VLSI, or related engineering discipline
5-8 years of hands-on experience in Physical Verification, with a focus on DRC, LVS, and Fill rule deck development
Deep proficiency with IC Validator, Calibre, Pegasus, or PVS, you have written and debugged complex runsets in at least one of these tools
Strong scripting skills in Perl, Tcl, and Python for automating PV workflows and qualification processes
Solid understanding of CMOS transistor layouts, ASIC design flows, and how layout decisions impact manufacturability and yield
Experience working with foundry PDKs and translating process rules into executable verification decks, exposure to sub-10nm nodes is a strong plus
Familiarity with competitive EDA tools and the ability to articulate technical tradeoffs between different PV platforms


Who You Are


You can take a 500-page foundry design manual and turn it into a working DRC deck without needing someone to hold your hand through every rule
When an LVS run fails with 10,000 errors, you do not panic, you filter the noise, find the root cause, and fix it systematically
You are comfortable on a call with a stressed customer two days before tapeout, walking them through a layout issue while keeping them confident the problem will get solved
Mentoring is not a checkbox for you, when a junior engineer asks a question, you take the time to explain the why, not just the what
You stay current on process technology and EDA tool developments because you are genuinely curious, not because it is in a performance goal
You can manage three runset development projects, two customer escalations, and a foundry qualification cycle at the same time without dropping threads or missing deadlines


The Team You'll Be Part Of

You will join a dynamic and diverse team of physical verification experts, tool developers, and field support engineers dedicated to enabling the next generation of semiconductor design. The team thrives on technical collaboration, innovation, and a shared commitment to delivering best-in-class PV solutions to customers worldwide. You will work closely with foundry partners, internal R&D, and field teams, ensuring that Synopsys remains a leader in EDA technology and customer satisfaction.

Rewards and Benefits

We offer a comprehensive range of health, wellness, and financial benefits to cater to your needs. Our total rewards include both monetary and non-monetary offerings. Your recruiter will provide more details about the salary range and benefits during the hiring process.
