Sr. Design Automation Engineer (AMS Flow Automation & Methodology)

We are seeking an AMS Flow Automation & Methodology Engineer to build and deploy scalable verification methodologies for analog and mixed-signal design teams. The role focuses on developing automated workflows, integrating EDA tools, enabling design signoff, and improving engineering productivity across complex semiconductor programs.

The ideal candidate combines strong analog/mixed-signal verification knowledge with software development and automation expertise to create robust, reusable solutions used by design teams worldwide.

Responsibilities


Develop and maintain automated verification workflows for Analog and Mixed-Signal designs.
Build methodology solutions around Cadence Virtuoso, Spectre/APS, and related AMS platforms.
Create flow automation frameworks for AC/DC/transient, noise, linearity, matching and corner/Monte Carlo coverage for analog and mixed-signal blocks.
Integrate design environments, databases, and simulation tools into streamlined user workflows.
Develop reusable automation utilities using Python, SKILL, Tcl, or Perl.
Partner with design, CAD, PDK, and software teams to improve productivity and quality.
Drive methodology adoption and provide technical support to internal and external customers.
Create reference flows, training materials, and best-practice documentation.
Develop regression, dashboard, and reporting infrastructure for design signoff.
Provide world-class user support and establish a gold-standard of driving customer success


Required Experience And Skills


M.Tech/M.E./M.S. in Electrical Engineering, Electronics Engineering, Computer Engineering, or related field.
3-5 years of experience in AMS methodology, CAD engineering, EDA applications engineering, or automation development.
Experience with Cadence Virtuoso, Spectre/APS, ADE Explorer/Assembler
Strong software development skills in Python and at least one of SKILL, Tcl, or Perl.
Understanding of AMS verification methodologies and design signoff requirements.
Experience developing production-quality automation and regression infrastructure.
Strong experience working with design data management and revision control systems like Perforce, DesignSync, Git etc.


Desired Experience And Skills


Experience in EDA product development or applications engineering.
Experience verifying designs in GaAs, SiGe HBT, or advanced CMOS RF processes
Familiarity with mixed-signal verification (real-number modeling, co-simulation, or AMS flows)
Knowledge of CI/CD, Jenkins, Git, containerized environments, or cloud-based EDA workflows.
