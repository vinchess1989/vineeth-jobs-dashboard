Rana Zawad Anwar
Technical Project Manager | Recruitment Lead | Turning Vision into Results | Skilled in High-Performance Environments

We are seeking a highly experienced and visionary Chief Technology Officer (CTO) to define the organization's technology vision, lead engineering strategy, and drive innovation across the business. This role requires a strong combination of technical expertise, executive leadership, strategic planning, and business alignment to ensure technology capabilities support long-term growth. With a strong background in technology leadership, software engineering, enterprise architecture, product development, digital transformation, and organizational scaling. You will play a critical role in shaping the company's technology roadmap, building high-performing engineering teams, driving technical excellence, and ensuring the delivery of secure, scalable, and innovative technology solutions.




Key Responsibilities:

1. Technology Vision & Strategic Leadership

Define and execute the organization's overall technology vision, strategy, and roadmap aligned with business objectives.
Provide executive leadership and strategic direction across software engineering,infrastructure, architecture, security, and technology operations.
Identify emerging technologies, industry trends, and innovation opportunities to maintain competitive advantage.
Establish technology standards, governance frameworks, and best practices across the organization.
Partner with CEO, executive leadership, and business stakeholders to translate business goals into technology initiatives.
Evaluate technology investments and ensure alignment with organizational priorities.
Drive digital transformation initiatives to improve operational efficiency, customer experience, and business scalability.
Establish a culture of innovation, technical excellence, accountability, and continuous improvement.
Act as the primary technology advisor for strategic business decisions.
Ensure technology strategies support current business needs while enabling future growth.




2. Engineering Leadership & Software Development Strategy

Lead and oversee software engineering, architecture, product engineering, and technical delivery teams.
Establish engineering practices that promote scalability, reliability, security, and high-quality software delivery.
Guide engineering leadership teams, including Directors of Engineering, Engineering Managers, and Technical Leads.
Define software development standards, architecture principles, and technical governance models.
Ensure adoption of modern engineering practices including Agile delivery, DevOps,CI/CD, automation, cloud-native development, and continuous improvement.
Review and approve major architectural decisions and technology implementations.
Drive improvements in software development lifecycle processes and engineeringproductivity.
Ensure engineering teams deliver solutions that meet business requirements, customer expectations, and technical standards.
Support the development of scalable platforms, products, and enterprise solutions.
Establish engineering metrics to measure delivery performance, quality, reliability, and innovation.




3. Technology Architecture & Innovation

Define enterprise technology architecture and long-term technical direction.
Ensure systems, applications, and platforms are scalable, secure, reliable, and maintainable.
Oversee architecture reviews, technology evaluations, and technical decision-making processes.
Promote adoption of cloud technologies, automation, artificial intelligence, data platforms, and modern software solutions.
Evaluate technology risks and implement strategies to improve system resilience.
Drive reduction of technical debt and improvement of engineering efficiency.
Establish standards for software architecture, integration patterns, security practices, and system performance.
Collaborate with engineering leaders to ensure technical solutions support business scalability.
Encourage research, experimentation, and adoption of innovative technology approaches.
Ensure technology infrastructure supports future product and business expansion.




4. Team Building & Engineering Organization Development

Build, mentor, and lead world-class technology teams.
Develop organizational structures that support scalability and business growth.
Recruit, retain, and develop senior engineering leaders and technical talent.
Participate in hiring decisions for engineering executives, senior engineers,architects, and contract technical resources.
Establish leadership development programs and technical career growth opportunities.
Create a culture of collaboration, ownership, innovation, and engineering excellence.
Coach technology leaders on strategic decision-making and organizational management.
Support workforce planning and resource allocation across technology teams.
Evaluate engineering capabilities and identify opportunities for improvement.
Promote knowledge sharing and continuous learning throughout the technology organization.




5. Business Collaboration & Executive Stakeholder Management

Collaborate closely with CEO, COO, CIO, CFO, Product, Sales, and Operations teams to align technology initiatives with business objectives.
Communicate technology strategy, progress, risks, and opportunities to executive stakeholders.
Provide technology insights that support business growth and competitive positioning.
Evaluate business requirements and translate them into effective technology solutions.
Partner with Product teams to define technical feasibility, delivery strategies, and product innovation.
Manage relationships with technology partners, vendors, and external service providers.
Present technology roadmaps, investment plans, and strategic recommendations to leadership teams.
Ensure technology decisions create measurable business value.
Balance innovation, operational stability, security, and cost efficiency.




6. Technology Budget & Resource Management

Develop and manage technology budgets aligned with organizational priorities.
Evaluate technology investments, infrastructure costs, and engineering resource requirements.
Approve technology spending, vendor partnerships, and strategic initiatives.
Optimize technology resources through effective workforce planning and capacity management.
Collaborate with Finance teams to evaluate return on technology investments.
Manage a balance between internal engineering teams and external technology resources.
Approve contractor requirements for specialized engineering skills and critical technology initiatives.
Ensure technology operations remain cost-effective and scalable.




7. Security, Compliance & Operational Excellence

Establish technology security strategies and ensure protection of company systems and data.
Collaborate with security teams to implement cybersecurity best practices.
Ensure compliance with industry standards, regulations, and technology policies.
Oversee technology risk management and disaster recovery planning.
Ensure high availability, reliability, and performance of technology systems.
Promote operational excellence through automation, monitoring, and continuous improvement.
Establish processes to identify and resolve technology risks proactively.
Ensure technology platforms support business continuity and long-term reliability.




Required Skills & Qualifications

Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, Information Technology, or a related technical field.
10+ years of experience in technology leadership, software engineering, and enterprise technology management.
Proven experience as CTO, VP of Engineering, Head of Technology, or similar executive technology leadership role.
Strong understanding of software architecture, cloud computing, enterprise systems, cybersecurity, and modern technology platforms.
Experience leading large-scale engineering teams and complex technology initiatives.
Strong strategic planning, business leadership, and executive communication skills.
Proven experience building engineering organizations and scaling technology operations.
Deep understanding of Agile, DevOps, CI/CD, software delivery, and engineering best practices.
Experience managing technology budgets, vendor relationships, and strategic technology investments.
Strong knowledge of emerging technologies and digital transformation strategies.
Ability to balance technical decisions with business objectives and financial considerations.
Experience working with global teams and enterprise-level technology environments.
