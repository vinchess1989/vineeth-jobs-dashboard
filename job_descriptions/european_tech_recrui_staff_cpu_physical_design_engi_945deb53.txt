Staff CPU Physical Design Engineer - ASIC / CPU Design / Physical Implementation / Semiconductor




We are currently partnered with a leading semiconductor technology company developing next-generation high-performance, low-power processors for advanced computing applications. As part of their continued growth, they are looking to hire a Staff CPU Physical Design Engineer to lead the implementation and optimisation of complex CPU and SoC designs, delivering world-class performance, power efficiency, and silicon quality.




Key responsibilities

Lead the physical implementation of high-performance CPU and ASIC blocks from RTL through to GDSII.
Drive the complete physical design flow including synthesis, floorplanning, power planning, placement, clock tree synthesis (CTS), routing, optimisation, engineering change orders (ECOs), and physical sign-off.
Develop and optimise high-performance, low-power CPU and SoC implementations to achieve aggressive power, performance, and area (PPA) targets.
Define implementation strategies, architecture constraints, and physical design methodologies for complex semiconductor products.
Perform static timing analysis (STA), timing closure, and physical verification across multiple operating modes and process corners.
Debug and resolve complex implementation challenges relating to timing, congestion, clocking, routing, signal integrity, power integrity, IR drop, electromigration (EM), and physical verification.




Key requirements

Bachelor's, Master's, or PhD in Electrical Engineering, Computer Engineering, Computer Science, or a related technical discipline.
Strong commercial experience in ASIC, CPU, or SoC physical design within the semiconductor industry.
Proven expertise across the complete ASIC implementation flow from RTL through GDSII.
Strong experience with synthesis, floorplanning, placement, CTS, routing, timing closure, ECO implementation, and physical sign-off.
Solid understanding of static timing analysis (STA), physical verification, and design optimisation techniques.
Extensive experience delivering high-performance CPU, SoC, or advanced ASIC products.
Experience with advanced semiconductor process nodes and complex physical implementation challenges.
Knowledge of Cadence Virtuoso, RTL-to-GDSII implementation flows, or equivalent semiconductor design environments.
