Meet the Team

Join Cisco's Silicon Operations team, where engineers collaborate with ASIC Design, Hardware, and Manufacturing partners to deliver high-quality silicon products from New Product Introduction (NPI) through high-volume production. The team drives innovation in test engineering, automation, and manufacturing excellence to ensure world-class product quality and reliability.

Your Impact

As a Test Engineering Leader, you will lead a team of test engineers responsible for developing, validating, and optimizing production test solutions for Cisco's next-generation ASIC and SoC products.

You Will


Lead the development and release of ATE test programs for ASIC/SoC products from NPI through production.
Partner with ASIC Design teams to bring up tests, characterize silicon, and improve test coverage.
Develop and debug test patterns, identify coverage gaps, and implement robust test solutions.
Drive high-volume manufacturing readiness by collaborating with Tier-1 OSAT partners to resolve production test and quality issues.
Lead automation initiatives to improve test efficiency, quality, and manufacturing scalability.
Analyze yield data, identify failure trends, and drive yield improvement and test time optimization.
Mentor and develop a high-performing team while fostering a culture of innovation, collaboration, and continuous improvement.
Partner with cross-functional engineering and manufacturing teams to ensure successful product launches.


Who You'll Work With

You'll collaborate closely with:


ASIC Design Teams (Central Hardware Group)
Silicon Operations Engineering Teams
New Product Introduction (NPI) Teams
Manufacturing and Operations Partners
Tier-1 OSAT Suppliers
Cross-functional Hardware and Product Engineering Teams


Minimum Qualifications


Bachelor's or Master's degree in Electrical Engineering, Computer Engineering, or related field.
12+ years of experience in ASIC/SoC NPI, ATE testing, silicon characterization, and production test.
Strong expertise with Advantest V93000 (SmarTest 7 & 8) platforms.
Proficiency in C, C++, Java, Perl, Python, and 93K test method development.
Experience leading technical teams and delivering complex engineering projects.


Preferred Qualifications


Strong knowledge of DFT methodologies, including Scan, ATPG, JTAG, MBIST, PVT, and SerDes.
Experience with yield improvement, failure analysis, and test time reduction.
Strong analytical and problem-solving skills.
Excellent communication, stakeholder management, and cross-functional leadership capabilities.
Experience working with global manufacturing partners and high-volume production environments.
Passion for automation, continuous improvement, and engineering innovation.
