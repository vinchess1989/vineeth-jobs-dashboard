Thomas Roscoe
Thomas Roscoe

Lead Technical Engineer (Cyber Security / SOC)

Location: Guildford / Frimley (Hybrid - 1 day a week)
Salary: £70,000 - £82,000 + 10% bonus
Security Clearance Eligibility Required




The Opportunity

We're supporting a leading technology and defence organisation as they build a brand-new Security Operations Centre (SOC) capability for a major international defence programme.




This is an opportunity for an experienced cyber security engineer to take ownership of the technical design and delivery of enterprise-scale SOC solutions. Working across both cloud and on-premise environments, you'll lead the design of cutting-edge cyber defence platforms, collaborate with customers and technical teams, and play a key role in protecting highly secure environments.




This role would suit someone with experience designing and implementing SOC technologies, SIEM platforms and enterprise security solutions who enjoys combining hands-on technical work with technical leadership.




Responsibilities

Lead the design and delivery of Security Operations Centre (SOC) solutions from high-level architecture through to deployment.
Produce High-Level and Low-Level Designs (HLDs & LLDs) for enterprise security platforms.
Design, configure and integrate SIEM, XDR and vulnerability management technologies.
Work across cloud and on-premise infrastructure, ensuring secure, scalable and resilient solutions.
Collaborate with customers, architects and engineering teams throughout the project lifecycle.
Support implementation, testing and deployment activities while ensuring technical quality and governance.
Provide technical leadership and mentoring to engineering teams throughout delivery.




Required Skills & Experience

Experience designing or implementing enterprise SOC or cyber security solutions.
Strong knowledge of SIEM platforms such as Microsoft Sentinel or Splunk.
Experience with technologies such as Microsoft Defender/XDR, EDR, SOAR or Vulnerability Management tools.
Strong understanding of networking fundamentals including TCP/IP, VLANs, routing, DNS and network security.
Experience working across Windows, Linux and cloud infrastructure (Azure, AWS or similar).
Knowledge of enterprise security architecture, threat detection and cyber defence principles.
Excellent stakeholder engagement and technical communication skills.
Experience working within highly regulated or secure environments is highly desirable.




Desirable

Experience with Cribl, Elastic, Tenable or similar security platforms.
Knowledge of Threat Intelligence and MITRE ATT&CK.
Experience producing technical design documentation.
Previous experience within Defence, Government or National Security environments.
Active SC or DV Clearance, or eligibility to obtain clearance.




What's on Offer

Work on nationally significant cyber security programmes.
Exposure to complex cloud and enterprise security environments.
Hybrid and flexible working.
Excellent career progression within a highly respected cyber and digital engineering organisation.
Competitive salary, bonus and comprehensive benefits package.
