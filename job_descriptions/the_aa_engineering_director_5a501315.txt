Company Description/ Business Unit

Location: London (hybrid working 3 office days per week)

Employment Type: Permanent, full time

Additional Benefits: Annual Bonus, Cash-Car Allowance & Private Medical Insurance

Think the AA is just about roadside assistance? Think again.

For over a century, we've been evolving and adapting. Today, as the nation's leading motoring organisation, we offer a wide range of products and services to millions of customers. From roadside assistance to home and motor insurance, and the latest driving technologies, we have it all. As we continue to expand, diversify, and modernise, joining us as an Engineering Director means you'll play a crucial role in our success and be part of this exciting motoring journey.

Our Chief Operating Office (COO) are the backbone of The AA, providing both stability and structure to support growth and innovation. We are the drivers of change.

This is the job

Build the engineering organisation that will power the future of the AA

Technology is fundamental to how we deliver exceptional experiences for millions of members and customers across the UK. As our new Engineering Director, you'll play a critical leadership role in transforming how engineering is delivered across the organisation, leading high performing teams that build secure, resilient and scalable technology at pace.

Reporting to the Chief Technology Officer, you'll translate technology strategy into execution, driving engineering excellence, modern delivery practices and AI enabled software engineering across the Chief Technology Office. This is a newly created executive role where you'll shape engineering capability, influence enterprise-wide technology decisions and help define the next chapter of our technology transformation.

What will I be doing?


Lead engineering delivery across the organisation, ensuring technology solutions are secure, scalable, resilient and aligned to strategic business priorities.
Drive modern engineering practices, AI enabled software development, automation and continuous improvement to increase delivery speed, quality and productivity.
Build and develop a high performing engineering organisation, creating an inclusive culture that attracts, develops and retains exceptional talent.
Partner with Technology Strategy, Product, Data, Digital and Operations leaders to deliver complex transformation programmes and maximise business value.
Establish engineering governance, standards and performance measures, providing assurance on delivery, operational resilience and engineering capability to Executive and Board stakeholders.
Champion innovation by identifying emerging technologies and engineering approaches that improve customer outcomes, operational performance and long-term value.


What do I need?


Significant experience leading large scale engineering organisations within complex enterprise environments.
A proven track record of delivering technology transformation, modern engineering practices and organisational change that delivers measurable business outcomes.
Strong technical expertise across cloud technologies, modern software engineering, platform engineering, integration and enterprise technology environments.
Experience developing engineering operating models, improving developer productivity and building high performing engineering teams.
Exceptional stakeholder management skills, with the ability to influence Executive and Board level leaders while working collaboratively across technology and business functions.
A strategic and commercially minded leader who combines technical credibility with the ability to inspire teams, challenge thinking and deliver lasting organisational impact.
