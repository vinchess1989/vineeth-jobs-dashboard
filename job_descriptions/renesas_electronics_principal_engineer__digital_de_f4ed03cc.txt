Job Description



Renesas has a growing presence in India with HC approaching 1000+ and significant presence with active government and university collaboration as well as OSAT footprint (JV with CG India). With the growing importance of India as a market (Growing semiconductor market and government goals / mandates of localization needs) and talent hub, our division’s (India for India) mission is to grow India market. We aspire to create products (SoCs, Software, Power and Analog chips etc) which serve needs for local market. Renesas is a leading electronics supplier globally, and this is a unique opportunity to directly influence the future products which will be offered to our customers in a new, fast growing and large Indian market with specific needs and applications.



We are seeking a highly motivated and innovative Principal IC Design Engineer (RTL Design) with strong experience in SoC development flows. In this role, you will play a pivotal part in the design and development of advanced integrated circuits that power Renesas MCU products. You will lead complex RTL design efforts, own key IP/subsystem deliverables, mentor junior engineers, and help evolve design methodologies and best practices.



Key Responsibilities



Lead RTL design and development of complex IPs and subsystems within MCU SoCs, ensuring compliance with performance, power, and area (PPA) targets
Drive micro-architecture definition and high-level digital design for assigned blocks
Support SoC integration, delivering high-quality subsystem views and documentation to Back-end and Design Verification teams
Own design quality checks, including lint, CDC, and RDC, and actively support functional verification of assigned blocks
Collaborate closely with system architects, verification engineers, physical design teams, and software teams to define specifications and ensure seamless end-to-end integration
Provide technical guidance and mentorship to junior engineers, raising overall team capability and design quality
Contribute to continuous improvement of RTL design methodologies, coding guidelines, and design processes


Qualifications



Required Qualifications



Bachelor’s degree in Electronics & Communication Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Computer Engineering, or related technical field
Minimum of 15 years of hands-on experience in digital IC/ASIC design, with a strong track record of delivering high-quality silicon
Strong expertise in RTL design using Verilog/VHDL
Solid understanding of digital design principles, timing analysis, clocking, resets, and verification methodologies
Experience using industry-standard EDA tools for synthesis, simulation, and static timing analysis
Strong problem-solving skills with the ability to own blocks end-to-end

Preferred Qualifications



Experience owning complex IPs or subsystem designs in large SoCs
Strong background in micro-architecture design and high-level digital design
Hands-on experience with RTL quality tools such as Spyglass (Lint, CDC, RDC)
Experience with low-power design techniques and methodologies
Familiarity with SoC-level design and integration
Understanding of synthesis flows and timing constraints
Scripting experience in Shell, Perl, Python or use of AI for design is a strong plus.
