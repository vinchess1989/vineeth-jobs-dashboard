At Cadence, we hire and develop leaders and innovators who want to make an impact on the world of technology.

Job Responsibilities


Be part of the product engineering team working on Power Noise Reliability analysis platform within Digital Sign-off Group at Cadence.
Build domain expertise in power integrity , 3DIC analysis & optimization , Industry’s first AI driven IR mitigation & fixing methodology , electrothermal optimization for digital designs.
Work closely with R&D to facilitate the development of these methodologies and flows.
Responsibilities also including supporting strategic customers and functional product engineering driving solutions in the Multiphysics space.


Minimum Qualifications


Bachelor’s Degree in; Electrical / VLSI Engineering with 3+ years related experience
OR Masters with relevant courses in digital systems in Electrical Engineering


Experience And Technical Skills Required


Coursework related to advanced digital logic design and advanced Very Large Scale Integrated-circuit (VLSI)
Fundamental understanding in Digital logic Design, CMOS logic Design, Power IR drop analysis, Circuit Design and Analysis, Digital and Behavioral simulations
Relevant coursework/projects or experience in physical design implementation flow using automatic placement and routing tool.
Ability to debug IR drop, Timing, and Electrical PDN issues in relation to IR and EM is a plus
Knowledge on Python or TCL scripting.


Behavioral Skills Required


Must possess strong written, verbal and presentation skills.
Ability to establish a close working relationship with both customer peers and management.
Explore what’s possible to get the job done, including creative use of unconventional solutions.
Work effectively across functions and geographies.
Push to raise the bar while always operating with integrity.
