Are you in?

Your role

Key responsibilities in your new role

As a Principal Engineer Digital Design, you will be at the forefront of developing cutting-edge CPU subsystems and non-volatile memory solutions for automotive applications. Working within our innovative team, you will drive technical excellence while shaping the future of semiconductor technology through architectural design and cross-functional collaboration.


Implement and/or review subblocks of CPU subsystems or non-volatile memories (NVM)
Contribute to the specification and definition of the microarchitecture of CPU subsystems or non-volatile memories for automotive applications
Ensure that digital design meets the requirements regarding functional safety and security
Drive technical decisions for your functional area and execute the implementation
Collaborate closely with hard macro designers, verification, digital design and firmware engineers to produce thoroughly verified, robust IP
Create innovative architecture by using the latest design techniques and tools
Provide technical guidance within the organization and support other departments


Your profile

Qualifications And Skills To Help You Succeed

You thrive in collaborative, international environments and bring innovative thinking to complex technical challenges. Your ability to communicate effectively across diverse teams will be key to driving success in this role.


A degree in electrical engineering, computer engineering or a related field of study
At least 8 years relevant working experience in defining and implementing complex digital circuits and functionalities
Experience in using the digital design flow including RTL/gate-level simulation, Linting, logic synthesis, STA, UPF (low power design) and DfT
Proficiency in HDLs like System Verilog or VHDL
Strong know-how in C and scripting languages like Perl or Python
Fluency in English (mandatory)
