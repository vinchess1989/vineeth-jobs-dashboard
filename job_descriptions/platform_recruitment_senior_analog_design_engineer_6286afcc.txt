Senior Analog IC Design Engineer




My client is an AI hardware startup developing cutting-edge accelerator technology to meet the growing performance and efficiency demands of AI infrastructure.




You'll play a key role in developing high-performance mixed-signal circuitry for an advanced SoC, collaborating across ASIC, photonics, and systems teams to take designs from concept through to silicon.




What you'll be doing:




Designing high-speed SerDes blocks including serializers, CDRs, TX/RX circuits, and clock generation
Performing schematic, post-layout, EM-IR, and reliability simulations
Developing behavioural and Verilog-A models for mixed-signal verification
Working with layout engineers to optimise floorplanning and implementation
Supporting silicon bring-up, lab validation, and performance debug




What they're looking for:




Strong analog or mixed-signal IC design experience
Hands-on experience designing high-speed SerDes/Pci-e
Expertise with Cadence Virtuoso, Spectre, and analog simulation flows
Solid understanding of signal integrity, jitter, equalisation, and high-speed interfaces
