About Axonne

Axonne Inc. is a fabless Silicon Valley semiconductor company developing cutting-edge high-speed networking solutions for the automotive and robotics industries. Our products enhance connectivity, data transfer, security, and system integration — enabling AI/ML applications and Software-Defined Networking in vehicles and robots.




Our Bucharest, Romania site expands Axonne's presence in Europe and serves as a hub for engineering innovation and product development, bringing together talented engineers working on next-generation technologies.




The Role

You will be a key member of Axonne’s R&D Digital ASIC Design team, developing the company’s core proprietary IP and next-generation silicon platforms. As a Senior ASIC Design Engineer, you will take end-to-end ownership of digital ASIC functional blocks and subsystems, from specification and RTL design through verification, silicon validation, and qualification. You will work closely with cross-functional teams and development partners to deliver robust, high-performance designs and help shape the technology roadmap.




Responsibilities

Lead the design and development of digital ASIC functional blocks and/or subsystems from architecture and RTL implementation through verification, silicon validation, and qualification.
Proactively collaborate with cross-functional teams and development partners to define and align on specifications, lead design reviews, resolve technical issues, and ensure comprehensive documentation.
Build and maintain strong relationships with internal and external development partners, driving effective collaboration and successful execution of joint development activities.
Serve as a key technical contributor to ASIC roadmaps, architecture evolution, and future product innovation.
Drive co-development activities, including prototyping ASIC designs on FPGA platforms and developing software models to enable pre-silicon firmware development, validation, and verification.
Identify and drive improvements in design methodologies, verification strategies, development processes, and EDA/tool automation to improve engineering productivity, quality, and time-to-market.




Required Qualifications

BS or MS degree in Electrical Engineering or equivalent, with a focus on VLSI design.
Minimum 5 years of ASIC or SoC design and development experience.
Deep expertise in embedded systems design, verification, and the end-to-end product development lifecycle.
Strong understanding of digital ASIC/SoC design methodologies and flows, from RTL development through synthesis, timing closure, verification, tape-out, and silicon characterization.
Extensive experience with industry-standard EDA tools, including Synopsys and Cadence platforms for simulation, synthesis, static timing analysis (STA), formal verification, and assertion-based verification; proficiency with Verilator for RTL simulation.
Strong expertise in UVM-based verification methodologies and scalable verification environments.
Proficiency in SystemVerilog and programming languages such as Python and C, with the ability to develop robust design, verification, and automation solutions.
Proficiency with Git and modern software development and version-control practices.
Excellent analytical, debugging, and problem-solving skills, with a demonstrated ability to drive complex technical issues to resolution.
Excellent written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to collaborate across hardware, software, verification, and systems teams and effectively communicate and negotiate technical trade-offs.
Ability to take ownership of complex technical problems, influence design decisions, and drive projects from concept through production.

Preferred Qualifications

Deep domain expertise in one or more of the following areas:
ARM-based processor and subsystem architectures.
AMBA protocols and specifications, including AXI, AXI-Stream (AXIS), and APB.
Application of AI/ML techniques to accelerate ASIC design, verification, automation, and overall engineering productivity.
Experience defining verification strategies, improving development methodologies, and driving improvements in engineering productivity and quality.
Experience working across multiple stages of the ASIC/SoC development lifecycle, from architecture and RTL through tape-out and post-silicon validation.

What We Offer

Competitive salary.
Real end-to-end ownership of your blocks, in a small and experienced team.




Axonne is an equal-opportunity employer. We value diversity and are committed to creating an inclusive environment for all employees.




This is an exciting opportunity to join a dynamic and fast-growing automotive semiconductor startup and contribute to the development of cutting-edge ASIC technology that powers the future of automotive E/E Architecture innovation. We look forward to receiving your application and welcoming you to our team!
