#WeAreIn for jobs that impact everyone's life. What if your ideas could change the way the world connects, powers up, or thinks? As a Principal Engineer Digital Design in our Research & Development team, you'll have the opportunity to merge creativity with your technical expertise by shaping the future of technology, driving groundbreaking projects, and bringing new ideas to life. Are you in?

Your role

Key responsibilities in your new role

As a Principal Engineer for Digital Design, you will be taking a leading role in the definition and deployment of processes and methodologies in the field of Digital IP Design.


Contribute substantially to the IP strategy in the field of Digital Design
Work as part of a team responsible for infrastructure, methodology and automation for IP Concept, Design and Verification
Facilitate and encourage innovation across boundaries, both internal and external to the IP design organisation
Observe and proactively act on external and internal trends
Coach & challenge new ideas, actively balance risks and opportunities and drive decision
Contribute to the evolution of traditional design practices towards the new wave of AI-based solutions for Digital Design


Your profile

Qualifications And Skills To Help You Succeed

You are a highly organized professional with excellent communication skills, able to clearly articulate ideas and proposals in both verbal and written formats while confidently influencing stakeholders. You manage your time efficiently, thrive when balancing multiple priorities, and consistently deliver results by staying focused, structured, and proactive in fast-paced environments.


A University Degree within STEM (Science, Technologies, Engineering and Mathematics) area
At least 9 years of relevant Digital Design experience from Digital IP design
An expert understanding of Digital Design Methodologies and flows
Proven ability to develop complex digital designs targeting high speed and/or low power, and an in-depth understanding of the SoC implementation flow
Proven ability to solve critical problems with high complexity by proactively involving expert networks, and to exploit deviations in order to support continuous improvement
Exposure to AI technologies and/or a strong interest in applying AI-based solutions to evolve traditional Digital Design methodologies and workflows
Fluency in English (mandatory)
