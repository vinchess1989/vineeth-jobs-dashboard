🚀 Hiring: Physical Design Engineer (BC Role) – 8+ Years




We are looking for an experienced Physical Design Engineer for a BC Role to join our growing VLSI team.




📍 Location: Bengaluru

💼 Experience: 8+ Years

⏳ Notice Period: Immediate to 30 Days Preferred




Key Responsibilities:




End-to-end Physical Design implementation from Netlist to GDSII.
Expertise in Floorplanning, Power Planning, Placement, CTS, Routing, and Physical Verification.
Perform timing closure across MMMC scenarios.
Optimize designs for Power, Performance, and Area (PPA).
Debug and resolve DRC/LVS, congestion, and Signal Integrity issues.
Perform ECO implementation and support tape-out activities.
Collaborate with RTL, Synthesis, STA, DFT, and Verification teams.




Required Skills:




Strong hands-on experience with Cadence Innovus or Synopsys ICC2/Fusion Compiler.
Expertise in Floorplanning, Placement, CTS, Routing, and Timing Closure.
Experience with MMMC, STA, SI, IR Drop, and EM Analysis.
Knowledge of advanced technology nodes (16nm/7nm/5nm/3nm).
Good scripting skills in TCL.




🎓 Qualification: B.E./B.Tech/M.E./M.Tech in ECE, EE, or a related field.
