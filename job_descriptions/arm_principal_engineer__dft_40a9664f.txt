Job Overview:

Arm's Central Engineering DFT Architecture and Methodology team defines the future of Design-for-Test across Arm's CPUs, GPUs, System IP, AI accelerators and next-generation SoCs. The team develops DFT architectures, methodologies and implementation flows that enable high-quality, scalable test solutions across soft IP, hard macros, test chips and complex multi-die SoCs.Working at the forefront of semiconductor innovation, you will collaborate with product teams, system architects, design engineers and EDA vendors to shape DFT technologies several years ahead of production. This is a highly technical architecture role with significant influence across Arm and the broader semiconductor ecosystem. 

Responsibilities:
As a Principal/Senior Principal DFT Architect/Engineer, you will:
Define DFT architecture for next-generation Arm IPs and SoCs, including emerging AI and hyperscale computing products.
Develop and drive scalable DFT methodologies and implementation flows used across multiple Arm engineering organizations.
Evaluate emerging DFT technologies and lead technical investigations to solve future product challenges.
Collaborate with architecture, implementation, verification and physical design teams to define robust DFT solutions.
Work closely with EDA vendors to influence product roadmaps, evaluate new capabilities and drive adoption of new technologies.
Produce architecture specifications, methodology documentation and deployment guidance.
Provide technical leadership and mentorship across project teams.
Support strategic engagements with customers, ASIC partners and industry standards bodies where appropriate.
Contribute to technical publications, patents and industry conferences.
Required Skills and Experience :
Principal or Senior Principal level engineer with extensive experience in Design-for-Test architecture and implementation.
Strong understanding of modern SoC DFT methodologies including:
Scan architecture and scan compression
ATPG and fault simulation
At-speed testing
Memory BIST
Logic BIST
JTAG / IEEE 1149.1
IJTAG / IEEE 1687
DFT verification and debug
Experience developing reusable DFT methodologies or automation rather than project-specific solutions.
Experience evaluating and deploying Siemens Tessent, Synopsys and/or Cadence DFT solutions.
Strong scripting skills (Perl, Tcl and/or Python) together with Linux/Unix expertise.
Excellent problem-solving and analytical skills.
Strong communication skills with the ability to influence technical direction across multiple engineering teams.
Degree (or equivalent) in Electronic Engineering, Computer Engineering or related discipline.
“Nice To Have” Skills and Experience :
Experience defining DFT architecture for large-scale SoCs.
Experience with heterogeneous compute systems, AI accelerators or hyperscale computing products.
Knowledge of IEEE 1500, IEEE 1838 and advanced IJTAG methodologies.
Experience with Streaming Scan Network (SSN) and chiplet/3DIC architectures.
Familiarity with silicon bring-up and production test.
Experience working directly with EDA vendors on new technology development.
Knowledge of synthesis, STA and physical implementation considerations for DFT.
Experience with formal verification and simulation methodologies.
Record of patents, conference publications or contributions to industry standards.
