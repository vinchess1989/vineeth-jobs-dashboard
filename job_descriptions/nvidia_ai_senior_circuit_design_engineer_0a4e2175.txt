NVIDIA has been transforming computer graphics, PC gaming, and accelerated computing for more than 25 years. It’s a unique legacy of innovation that’s fueled by great technology—and amazing people.

Today, we’re tapping into the unlimited potential of AI to define the next era of computing. An era in which our GPU acts as the brains of computers, robots, and self-driving cars that can understand the world. Doing what’s never been done before takes vision, innovation, and the world’s best talent. As an NVIDIAN, you’ll be immersed in a diverse, supportive environment where everyone is inspired to do their best work. Come join the team and see how you can make a lasting impact on the world.

This is a phenomenal opportunity to contribute to innovative technology in a role that significantly influences silicon performance. As a part of our world-class engineering team, you will help craft the future of AI and accelerated computing, working alongside very hard-working engineers dedicated to groundbreaking innovations.

What You'll Be Doing


Be part of a hardworking, talented, multi-functional Global Circuits Team, designing and delivering groundbreaking circuit IPs involving SRAM arrays, Analog and/or Digital circuits.
Work on architecting innovative circuit solutions and methodologies which supervise, measure on-chip silicon performance parameters and close the gap to pre-silicon estimations for logic and RAMs
Be involved in design and verification of Custom Circuit IPs to improve the power, performance, silicon correlation and security of NVIDIA CPU, GPU and Networking chips
Participate in discussion with various teams to understand challenges related to Vmin, Vmax, VF, Yield, spice modelling, silicon debug and propose innovative solutions.
Understanding of existing circuit methodologies for logic/RAMs and suggest improvements.
This role includes interacting with several global teams across geographies, understanding various methodologies and improving silicon correlation of logic and RAM circuits.
Be a mentor/technical lead for junior team members.


What We Need To See


Have sound fundamentals of CMOS devices, RC circuits, timing concepts involving dynamic and sequential circuits.
Hands-on experience with circuit design, spice simulations, statistical modelling, Static/Dynamic timing analysis
Knowledge of silicon corelation, data analytics and physical design is a huge plus.
Ability to close design to the specs by running and developing various flows like EM, IR, Noise, and static timing analysis (STA)
Good communication skills ; should be an excellent teammate.
BE/M-Tech in Electrical & Electronics or equivalent experience.
3-8 years of experience
