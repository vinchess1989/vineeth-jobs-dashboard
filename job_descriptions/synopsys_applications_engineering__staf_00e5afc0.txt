Job Description and Requirements

We Are

Synopsys is the leader in engineering solutions from silicon to systems, enabling customers to rapidly innovate AI-powered products. We deliver industry-leading silicon design, IP, simulation and analysis solutions, and design services. We partner closely with our customers across a wide range of industries to maximize their R&D capability and productivity, powering innovation today that ignites the ingenuity of tomorrow.

You Are

You have spent years deep in the semiconductor world, close enough to the metal to know that timing closure at 3nm is not a checkbox, it is a negotiation with physics. You have debugged power delivery networks that looked fine on paper but failed in silicon, and you learned early that the best solution is the one the customer can actually use on Monday morning.

You are the kind of engineer who can walk into a room full of stressed design leads at a top foundry, look at their STA violations or EMIR hotspots, and know within ten minutes whether this is a tool issue, a methodology gap, or a design problem they have not named yet. You do not wait to be handed a perfect problem statement. You ask the right questions, triangulate fast, and move toward a solution even when half the data is missing.

Working directly with customers energizes you. Not because you love travel or presentations, but because you care about making complex tools do what they are supposed to do in real design environments. You have a point of view on what good signoff looks like, and you are not afraid to push back when a flow does not make sense or a feature request misses the actual problem.

At Synopsys, you will work with the teams building the tools and the customers depending on them to tape out. What you learn in Austin this week will shape what ships globally next quarter.

What You'll Be Doing

Deploy and support Synopsys signoff tools including PrimeTime, RedHawk-SC, PrimeClosure, PTECO, Tweaker, and StarRC at leading semiconductor companies and foundries working on advanced node SoCs and 3DICs
Engage directly with customer design teams to diagnose and resolve static timing analysis, power integrity, signal integrity, and reliability signoff challenges across chip, package, and system levels
Own technical issues end to end, working proactively with assigned accounts to deliver solutions that unblock tapeouts and keep development schedules on track
Collaborate with Synopsys product development teams to translate customer pain points and feature requests into actionable product enhancements and roadmap priorities
Support presales technical activities by demonstrating the Seascape platform and signoff solutions, helping sales teams articulate value and close opportunities
Build and maintain a knowledge base of EDA best practices, deployment guides, and troubleshooting FAQs for use by both customers and internal support teams
Participate in cross-functional initiatives within Synopsys, sharing what you learn in the field to improve tools, methodologies, and customer engagement strategies

The Impact You Will Have

Accelerate time to tapeout for customers by resolving complex STA, PDN, and reliability issues that would otherwise stall multi-million dollar design programs
Shape the next generation of Synopsys signoff products by feeding real-world design challenges and customer requirements directly into engineering teams
Expand adoption of the Seascape platform and advanced signoff flows across top-tier semiconductor companies, strengthening Synopsys' position in the signoff market
Build deep technical trust with customer engineering teams, turning one-time support interactions into long-term strategic partnerships
Reduce escalations and support load by creating reusable solutions, best practices, and documentation that help customers and internal teams solve problems faster
Enable successful deployment of Synopsys tools at advanced nodes where margins are tight and traditional methods break down
Contribute to revenue growth by supporting presales efforts with technical credibility and solution-oriented customer engagement

What You'll Need

3-5 years of hands-on experience in semiconductor design, with deep expertise in static timing analysis, timing closure, ECO implementation, or power delivery network analysis at block or SoC level
Proven ability to provide technical support or applications engineering for timing signoff or power integrity domains, including direct customer interaction and issue resolution
Working proficiency with Synopsys signoff tools such as PrimeTime, RedHawk-SC, PrimeClosure, PTECO, Tweaker, or StarRC in real design environments
Strong scripting and programming skills in languages like Python, Tcl, or Perl to automate workflows, analyze results, and build custom solutions for customer problems
Experience with PDN implementation flows, advanced node design challenges, or 3DIC methodologies is a strong plus

Who You Are

You can take a vague customer complaint like "timing is broken" and within an hour of log review and a few targeted questions, identify whether it is a constraint issue, a tool setting, or an actual design flaw
Comfortable presenting technical content to both hands-on engineers and engineering directors, you adjust your depth and framing depending on who is in the room without losing clarity
You do not need to be the smartest person in the room, but you do need to be the one who connects the dots between what the tool is doing and what the customer actually needs
Organized enough to juggle multiple customer engagements, internal product discussions, and knowledge base contributions without dropping threads or losing context
You treat customer problems like they are your problems, not because it is your job, but because you genuinely want to see the design succeed and the tool work the way it should
Willing to travel as needed to customer sites, understanding that some problems only make sense when you are sitting next to the engineer who hit them
