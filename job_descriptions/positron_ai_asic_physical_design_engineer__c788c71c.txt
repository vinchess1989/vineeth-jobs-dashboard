ASIC Physical Design Engineer, Senior

About Positron AI

Positron AI is building next-generation AI inference accelerators designed from the ground up for low-latency, high-throughput large language model inference. Our first-generation ASIC, Asimov, is a cutting-edge accelerator targeting frontier AI workloads, with additional generations already underway.

Role Overview
Contribute to the physical implementation of Positron's AI accelerators from floorplanning through tapeout. Support and help drive implementation methodology while partnering with RTL, synthesis, DFT, package, and backend design services to achieve world-class PPA.




Key Responsibilities

Physical Implementation

Support floorplanning, macro placement, and power planning across Positron's AI accelerator designs.
Drive clock tree synthesis, place and route, and routing optimization through to timing closure on assigned blocks.
Contribute to signal integrity, IR/EM closure, ECO implementation, and metal fill to deliver signoff-quality results.
Support tapeout signoff for physical implementation deliverables.

Methodology Development

Contribute to methodology for power grids, power/ground tiles, and bump planning.
Assist in building congestion analysis and wire estimation approaches to support early floorplan decisions.
Support partitioning and hierarchical implementation strategies for complex, multi-block designs, under guidance from senior/principal leads.

Tools & Technical Execution

Execute Innovus-based implementation flows for assigned blocks.
Apply hands-on expertise across Innovus, ICC2, Tempus, PrimeTime, Voltus, RedHawk, and Calibre.

Closure & Signoff

Drive closure across timing, power, area, congestion, DRC, LVS, EM, IR, and yield for owned blocks.

Cross-Functional Partnership

Partner closely with backend design services to align on implementation strategy and execution.
Participate in implementation quality reviews.

AI-Enabled Engineering

Apply AI-enabled techniques for automated QoR analysis, congestion prediction, routing prediction, and floorplan optimization.




Required Qualifications

5–8 years of experience in physical design, spanning floorplanning through tapeout.
Experience contributing to one or more advanced-node tapeouts.
Strong proficiency in Innovus and/or ICC2 implementation flows.
Solid working knowledge of physical implementation, including floorplanning, clock tree synthesis, timing, and power closure.
Experience supporting signoff across timing, power, and related quality metrics.




Preferred Qualifications

Experience with AI accelerator design.
Familiarity with large SRAM arrays.
Experience with SerDes and LPDDR interfaces.
Exposure to chiplet architectures and 2.5D advanced packaging.
