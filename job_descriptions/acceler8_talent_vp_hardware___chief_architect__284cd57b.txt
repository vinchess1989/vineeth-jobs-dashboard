𝗩𝗣 𝗛𝗮𝗿𝗱𝘄𝗮𝗿𝗲 / 𝗖𝗵𝗶𝗲𝗳 𝗔𝗿𝗰𝗵𝗶𝘁𝗲𝗰𝘁 - 𝗦𝘁𝗲𝗮𝗹𝘁𝗵 𝗔𝗜 𝗜𝗻𝗳𝗲𝗿𝗲𝗻𝗰𝗲 𝗖𝗵𝗶𝗽 𝗦𝘁𝗮𝗿𝘁𝘂𝗽




We're hiring a VP Hardware / Chief Architect to lead the silicon programme for an early-stage AI company emerging from a top AI research lab at Stanford. As VP Hardware, you’ll define the architecture and technical roadmap for a purpose-built LLM inference ASIC designed to deliver high token throughput within a low-power envelope. This isn’t a conventional executive position. Success depends on deep digital-silicon expertise, repeated tapeout ownership, and the willingness to remain directly involved in microarchitecture, RTL, verification, and bring-up.




The founding team has developed an inference ASIC optimized for the two workloads where general-purpose GPUs struggle most: high-throughput batched serving, where cost per token determines the economics, and low-latency interactive inference, where tokens per second per user defines the experience. In the team’s current benchmarks against an NVIDIA B200, the chip delivers more than 30,000 tokens per second, approximately 20x better energy efficiency per token, and more than an order of magnitude lower cost per token.




The company is designing the hardware, runtime, and serving system together rather than treating the accelerator as an isolated component. The broader thesis is that the next major gains in AI will come from maximizing intelligence per watt, not simply increasing raw compute. The founding group combines researchers from a top Stanford AI lab, veteran chip architects, and experienced hardware entrepreneurs building the company from the ground up.




As VP Hardware / Chief Architect, you’ll own the journey from architectural specification through tapeout, bring-up, validation, and production. You’ll work directly with the founders to translate an ambitious inference-systems thesis into a scalable silicon roadmap, while building the hardware organization required to deliver it. The role combines company-level technical leadership with direct influence over the most important design decisions.

𝗞𝗲𝘆 𝗿𝗲𝘀𝗽𝗼𝗻𝘀𝗶𝗯𝗶𝗹𝗶𝘁𝗶𝗲𝘀:




• Define and own the full-chip architecture for a purpose-built LLM inference ASIC

• Lead the programme from specification through tapeout, bring-up, and production

• Make and review critical microarchitecture, RTL, and verification decisions

• Own performance, power, latency, throughput, and cost trade-offs

• Build the silicon team, hiring plan, engineering standards, and technical culture

• Develop the roadmap across first-generation silicon and future architectures

• Work closely with software and ML systems teams on hardware-software co-design

• Manage technical relationships with foundry, EDA, IP, and engineering partners

• Identify and retire architectural and execution risk before tapeout

• Remain a hands-on technical authority as the wider organization scales




You’ll work across AI accelerators, digital ASIC architecture, SoC design, RTL, design verification, performance modeling, power optimization, memory systems, tapeout, silicon bring-up, and hardware-software co-design. This VP Hardware / Chief Architect role offers the chance to build the architecture, team, and company behind a new approach to serving AI, starting from first principles rather than inheriting an established roadmap.
