About Us

Adeia Inc. (Nasdaq: ADEA) the technology company known for developing foundational innovations that enable next-generation solutions for the semiconductor and media industries, headquartered in San Jose, CA, with approximately 150 employees, primarily in North America. Adeia invents, develops, and licenses fundamental innovations that shape the way millions of people explore and experience entertainment in an increasingly connected world. From TVs to smartphones, and across all types of entertainment experiences, Adeia’s technologies allow users to manage content and connections in a way that is smart, immersive, and personal. Adeia is proud to be recognized by U.S. News & World Report as one of the “Best Companies to Work For" in 2025 - 2026.




Position Summary




The Semiconductor 3D Architecture, Director provides enterprise technical leadership in memory and logic integration, defining the long-term technology strategy for one of Adeia’s key semiconductor innovation areas. The role establishes and leads the company’s memory logic innovation program, identifying strategic technology investments, guiding cross-functional R&D, and translating emerging technologies into differentiated semiconductor platforms and high-value IP licensing opportunities. Working closely with the VP, Emerging Technology & Discovery, this leader shapes Adeia’s technical direction in memory logic architectures while building strategic partnerships across the semiconductor ecosystem. The role serves as the company’s recognized technical authority in memory logic integration, influencing engineering priorities, technology strategy, industry collaboration and future innovation platforms.







Key Responsibilities




Technology Strategy & Innovation

Own the Memory Logic Innovation Program. Establish and lead Adeia’s semiconductor strategy for memory logic integration, defining long-term technology roadmaps, innovation priorities, and strategic investment areas aligned with the company’s semiconductor strategy.
Build a New Semiconductor Technology Vertical. Identify, evaluate, and mature high-impact technologies into differentiated semiconductor platforms that expand Adeia’s technology portfolio and create future IP licensing opportunities.
Drive Technology Portfolio Decisions. Evaluate emerging technologies, competitive landscapes, and market opportunities and guide strategic technology decisions to prioritize innovation investments and industry collaborations.




Architecture Leadership & Technology Development

Lead Computer Architecture Innovation. Direct the development of novel device, circuit, package, and system architectures that address complex memory logic challenges and create differentiated technical capabilities.
Drive Cross-Layer Technology Development. Lead architectural exploration across transistor physics, standard cell design, circuit implementation, physical layout, place and route, memory and logic subsystem design, and system architecture to identify breakthrough innovation opportunities.
Direct Technology Validation. Oversee modeling, simulation, prototype development, and technical validation activities to mature concepts into scalable technology platforms and future licensing opportunities.




IP Leadership & External Engagement

Lead Strategic IP Development. Direct invention strategy and partner with IP engineering and patent counsel to build foundational patent portfolios supporting long-term licensing opportunities.
Develop Strategic Industry Partnerships. Build executive-level technical relationships with semiconductor companies, customers, foundries, EDA partners, universities, research organizations, and technical consultants to jointly explore emerging technologies, develop prototypes, and validate future architectures.
Represent Adeia Externally. Serve as Adeia’s technical authority in customer engagements, industry forums, technical collaborations, and strategic partnerships to strengthen the company’s leadership in emerging semiconductor technologies.




Enterprise Technology Leadership

Drive Enterprise Technology Alignment. Align Discovery Engineering, R&D, IP Engineering, Product Engineering, and executive leadership around memory logic innovation priorities, ensuring coordinated execution of the technology roadmap.
Guide Technical Direction Across the Organization. Influence architectural decisions, technology standards, and innovation priorities across engineering organizations while mentoring senior technical contributors and fostering technical excellence without direct managerial responsibility.
Shape Future Innovation Programs. Identify emerging opportunities and develop proposals for new technology initiatives that strengthen Adeia’s long-term semiconductor strategy and competitive differentiation.




Key Competencies




Demonstrates strategic thinking and long-term business perspective.
Builds credibility through deep technical expertise and sound judgment.
Influences and collaborates effectively across organizations without direct authority.
Drives innovation through curiosity, creativity, and continuous learning.
Builds trusted relationships internally and across the semiconductor ecosystem.




Minimum Qualifications




Master’s or PhD in Electrical Engineering, Computer Engineering, Physics, or a related field.
15+ years of experience in semiconductor architecture, memory systems, advanced logic design, or related technologies.
Deep expertise in memory logic integration, transistor technologies, and physical implementation.
Significant experience in standard cell design, circuit layout, and place and route.
Experience defining technology roadmaps and evaluating emerging semiconductor technologies.
Experience developing foundational inventions, patents, or advanced semiconductor technology concepts.
Experience leading cross-functional technical programs and influencing engineering strategy across multiple organizations.
Experience collaborating with semiconductor companies, customers, foundries, or ecosystem partners to develop and validate advanced technology concepts.







Preferred Qualifications




Experience with heterogeneous integration, advanced packaging, or 3D integration.
Experience in compute-in-memory, memory-in-compute, or advanced memory architectures.
Experience working closely with IP engineering and patent development.
