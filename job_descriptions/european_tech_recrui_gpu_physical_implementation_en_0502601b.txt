GPU Physical Implementation Engineer




A leading technology company is looking for a GPU Physical Implementation Engineer to join its graphics engineering organisation and work on the implementation, verification, performance, and power optimisation of next-generation GPU cores.




This is an opportunity to work across the SoC physical implementation flow, collaborating with architects, circuit designers, verification engineers, and software teams to deliver GPU designs that meet demanding performance, power, and area (PPA) targets.




You’ll contribute across the implementation lifecycle, including synthesis, place and route, STA, power analysis, circuit design, process technology evaluation, and pre/post-silicon verification.




Ideal candidates will have:

• Master's or PhD in Electrical Engineering, Computer Engineering, Computer Science, or related field

• 2+ years of relevant GPU engineering experience

• Strong experience with P&R / PnR and synthesis tools, flows, and methodologies

• Experience with SoC physical implementation and design methodologies

• Understanding of semiconductor process technology and technology nodes

• Knowledge of circuit design and semi-custom design methodologies

• Strong Static Timing Analysis (STA) experience

• Experience with power analysis and optimisation

• Experience balancing GPU performance, power, and functionality

• Ability to analyse benchmarks and test results to identify performance and power issues

• Experience working cross-functionally with hardware, software, architecture, and verification teams

• Strong technical problem-solving and analytical skills

• Ability to work effectively with ambiguous and complex technical requirements

• Strong technical documentation and communication skills

Preferred Qualifications:

• Experience with GPU architecture or graphics hardware

• Pre- and post-silicon GPU validation and verification

• Graphics workloads, benchmarking, and performance analysis

• Semi-custom circuit implementation

• Experience across multiple process technology nodes

• Experience developing or improving physical design flows and methodologies

• Knowledge of industry trends in GPU and graphics technology

• Experience working with third-party vendors and external engineering partners
