General Summary:

3 to 15 years of solid experience in IP/Sub System/SoC RTL design and micro-architecture

Understanding of bus protocols like CHI/AHB/AXI/ACE/ACE-Lite and Interconnect concepts

Good knowledge of Verilog RTL development and VLSI Digital Design flows

Knowledgeable on ARM / RISCV system architecture, Memory Management, Virtual Memory concepts, Core sight architecture, power management fundamentals

Understanding of multi-core ARMv8/v9 CPU architecture, coherency protocols and virtualization

Working knowledge of Lint, CDC, PLDRC, CLP, Synthesis, DFT, verification, formal verification, silicon debug

Good understanding of the design convergence cycle in terms of architecture, micro-architecture, synthesis, timing closure and verification

Manage SoC dependencies, planning and tracking of all front-end design related tasks

Driving project milestones across the design, verification and physical implementations

Communication and collaboration skills to work with a large world-wide design organization

Proficiency in Gen AI skills, Scripting languages (Python or Perl) for Automation initiatives is desirable

Minimum Qualifications:

• Bachelor's degree in Electronics Engineering, or related field and 2+ years of Hardware Engineering or related work experience
OR

Master's degree in VLSI / Micro Electronics or related field and 1+ year of Hardware Engineering or related work experience.
OR

PhD in Electronics Engineering or related field

Minimum Qualifications:

• Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Electrical/Electronics Engineering, Engineering, or related field and 4+ years of Hardware Engineering or related work experience.
OR
Master's degree in Computer Science, Electrical/Electronics Engineering, Engineering, or related field and 3+ years of Hardware Engineering or related work experience.
OR
PhD in Computer Science, Electrical/Electronics Engineering, Engineering, or related field and 2+ years of Hardware Engineering or related work experience.
