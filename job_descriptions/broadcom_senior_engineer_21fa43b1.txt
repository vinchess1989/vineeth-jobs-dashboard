Job Description:

In this role, the Engineer will apply and lead Broadcom's proven design methodology and milestone flow to meet rigorous criteria for achieving Right-First-Time silicon. Candidates must have extensive experience in block-level and top-level layout activities, including floor-planning, partitioning, placement, clock tree synthesis, routing, and physical verification.

Responsibilities include, but are not limited to:

  - Understanding SoC for top-down/bottom-up physical design integration in 5nm and lower technologies.
  - Demonstrating deep functional knowledge of P&R flows, with the ability to quickly adapt to internal methodologies.
  - Implementing timing and functional ECOs.
  - Executing P&R, extraction, physical verification, and working toward STA closure.
  - Building new automation flows as needed and adapting existing flows for re-use.
  - Maintaining proficiency in Cadence/Synopsys/Mentor EDA tools for P&R, PV, STA, and ECO.
  - Developing automation with high expertise in industry-standard programming languages.
  - Clearly understanding execution requisites and leading by example.
  - Driving tangible improvements in turnaround time (TAT) alongside better quality.

Minimum Qualifications:

  - MSEE/MSCS with 6+ years of experience, or BSEE/BSCS with 8+ years of experience.
  - Deep understanding of backend digital design flows.
  - Proficiency in timing and physical constraints.
  - Proficiency in handling EDA tools across floorplan, partition, placement, CTS, and route stages for SoC TOP.
  - Proficiency with backend EDA tools such as Genus, Innovus, Quantus, Tempus, DC, Star-RCXT, PT, PrimeRail, Voltus, Redhawk.
  - Proficiency in Tcl and Perl.
  - Excellent analytical skills.
  - Proven ability to collaborate in a multi-functional environment across different sites and time zones.
