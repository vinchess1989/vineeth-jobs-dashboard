I recruit ASIC Design Engineers, Design Verification Engineers and Physical Design Engineers across Europe.

Physical Design Lead - Barcelona




I have an exciting new opportunity with a client looking to grow their team in Barcelona. They are looking for an experienced Physical Design Engineer to Lead the team. This is a fantastic opportunity to work on complex projects and RISC-V IP. They have some huge growth plans for the next few years, so now is a great to join to watch the business grow, and with that will naturally come career progression.




Requirements:

Bachelors or Masters in Electronics or a related field
Knowledge of the entire PD flow from netlist to GDS (Floorplanning, Power planning, Placement & Optimisation, CTS, Routing, Post route optimisation and DRC closure).
Experience in logic synthesis, conformal, static timing analysis and Place and Route (PnR)
Previous experience in a technical lead role
