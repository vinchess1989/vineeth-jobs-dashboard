This role is for one of the Weekday's clients

Min Experience: 9+ years

Location: Bengaluru

JobType: full-time

We are looking for an experienced Lead SoC Architect to define and drive the architecture of next-generation semiconductor products. You will lead architecture definition, subsystem integration, and technical decision-making for complex SoCs targeting AI, GPU, networking, multimedia, RISC-V, and datacenter applications.

Requirements

Key Responsibilities


Define top-level SoC architecture and subsystem partitioning
Drive architecture trade-offs across performance, power, area, latency, and bandwidth
Develop architecture specifications and interface definitions
Collaborate with RTL, Verification, Physical Design, Firmware, Software, and System teams
Perform architecture modeling and workload analysis using C/C++/SystemC
Define memory hierarchy, NoC, coherency architecture, and cache strategy
Integrate third-party IPs including CPU, GPU, NPU, PCIe, DDR/LPDDR, Ethernet, Multimedia, and Security IP
Support verification planning, silicon bring-up, debug, and performance optimization
Contribute to future product and technology roadmaps


Required Qualifications


Bachelor's or Master's degree in Electrical, Computer Engineering, Computer Science, or related field
10+ years of experience in SoC Architecture, ASIC Design, or complex semiconductor systems
Strong expertise in SoC architecture and subsystem integration
Experience with CPU/GPU/NPU architectures, NoC, Cache Coherency (CHI/ACE/CXL), PCIe, DDR5/LPDDR5X, RTL methodologies, and architecture modeling
Strong analytical, communication, and leadership skills


Preferred Qualifications


AI Accelerator, GPU, Networking, RISC-V, or Datacenter SoC experience
Chiplet or Multi-die architecture
FPGA prototyping and emulation
Linux Drivers or System Software
Technical leadership across cross-functional engineering teams


Must-have Skills

SoC Architecture, ASIC Design, Performance Modeling

Good-to-have Skills

RISC-V, Cache Coherency, NoC
