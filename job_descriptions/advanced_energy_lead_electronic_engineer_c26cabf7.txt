About Advanced Energy
Advanced Energy Industries, Inc. (NASDAQ: AEIS) empowers innovation across the world’s most advanced semiconductor and industrial applications. We design and manufacture precision power and control solutions that enable breakthrough technologies in thin film deposition, industrial manufacturing, and medical power systems. With a proud heritage, award winning technology, and a global workforce, Advanced Energy is committed to fostering innovation, collaboration, and career growth.

Position Summary:
We are seeking a highly skilled and driven Lead Engineer to create new-to-world technology and differentiated products within the medical industry. Advanced Engineering in Cork is focused on developing next-generation concepts for Electrosurgery generators, including pulsed electric field technologies, ablation treatments, and other surgical energy applications. This role also encompasses exploring high-efficiency power supply architectures for advanced applications in industry.

Essential Responsibilities:
Research & Innovation:

Contribute to the conception and development of advanced electrosurgical generators and related technologies
Explore and develop emerging technologies in power electronics, surgical energy delivery, and high-efficiency power conversion
Translate research insights into practical prototypes and future product concepts



Product Design:

Design and analysis of systems products and subsystems/circuits to specification, cost, budget, schedule and quality goals
Drive critical thinking and problem-solving across complex engineering challenges
Assumes responsibility for the success of the technology readiness and the delivery of proven proof-of-concept platforms
Engage proactively with customers to understand the product/system in the customers’ applications and recommend/implement corrective actions or enhancements as needed
Evaluate and implement cutting-edge technologies for surgical energy applications
Collaborate with Product Development teams to review and transfer technology to enable the successful development and launch of new products
Collaborate with departments, such as New Product Implementation, purchasing, quality, program management, finance, safety, production, etc.
Ensure compliance with regulatory and safety standards for power solutions for medical devices
Provide expert guidance on power electronics design, control strategies, and system integration



Team Leadership

Lead, mentor, and inspire a multidisciplinary team of engineers
Drive career development through coaching, training, and performance management
Cultivate a culture of innovation, collaboration, and technical excellence



Work Environment:

Location: Cork – on site
Works in a standard office and lab environment
Domestic and International travel of up to 10%
Interfaces with internal & external customers in all functional areas



Technical and Professional Skills:

Proven track record of developing power electronics prototypes and products
Experience with influencing and negotiating in complex situations
Advanced knowledge of power conversion topologies, high-frequency switching, and control systems
Strategic thinker with a passion for innovation and technology exploration
Ability to solve complex problems independently
Strong coaching and mentoring skills
Well organized and disciplined
Strong team player skills and mindset
Ability to thrive in a fast-paced, R&D environment
Familiarity with electrosurgical technologies, pulsed electric fields, or ablation systems is highly desirable



Experience/Education:
Essential:

Minimum bachelor’s degree in electronic engineering or similar field. Ideally, a PhD or master’s degree in electrical engineering, power electronics, or related field
Minimum 8+ years in highly technical design and development, preferably in medical or high-reliability systems
Show a progression of technical responsibilities and creation of IP in previous roles



What AE Offers:

Opportunity to work on cutting edge technologies in the medical industry and beyond
A collaborative, innovative, and inclusive work environment.
Competitive compensation and benefits.
Global and local career development opportunities.
