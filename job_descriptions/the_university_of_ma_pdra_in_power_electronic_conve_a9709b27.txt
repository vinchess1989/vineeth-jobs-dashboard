We are seeking a motivated and collaborative individual to join our team as a Postdoctoral Research Associate in Power Systems and Converter Modelling. This role offers an exciting opportunity to contribute to research supporting Britain’s transition towards a cleaner, more sustainable electric power network. You will be based in the Department of Electrical Engineering at the University of Manchester and work as part of a wider pan-university community with strong industrial involvement.

You will be responsible for:


Reviewing and assessing emerging inverter-based resource and HVDC technologies, including their costs, benefits, technical capabilities and implications for future power system operation.
Creating appropriate dynamic models of the power network, IBRs and their control for EMT and other time-stepping simulation systems.
Investigating and evaluating local, supplementary, and system-level controls for inverter-based resources and HVDC systems for variety of use cases.
Liaising with project investigators, collaborators, and industrial partners.



We welcome candidates who bring diverse perspectives, experiences, and approaches to their work.

About You

We encourage applications from individuals with a wide range of backgrounds and experiences. You should demonstrate:

Essential Criteria


Have a relevant PhD (or an equivalent combination of education and industrial experience) in power systems incorporating power electronics.
Expertise in research focussed on the integration of large-scale power electronic devices (e.g. HVDC) within power systems involving dynamic modelling, stability analysis and simulation.



Desirable Criteria


Experience with EMT simulation of power systems and power electronic converters using PSCAD/EMTDC.



We value transferable skills and real-world experience as much as formal qualifications.
