Job Title

Principal Design Engineer – Temporary Works




Summary

Our client is seeking an experienced Principal Design Engineer to join their team in Watford. This role is ideal for someone with a deep understanding of temporary works and civil engineering who can provide technical leadership and innovative solutions. You’ll have the opportunity to make an impact on complex infrastructure projects and shape the future of construction.




Location and Working Pattern

Watford (hybrid arrangements to be discussed with the client but the initial induction will entail 3 months 100% office presence)




Client Overview

The client is a market leader in the UK construction and engineering sector, delivering major infrastructure projects that drive progress, innovation, and sustainability. Their projects make a tangible difference to communities and the environment, making this an exciting time to join their team.




Responsibilities

Lead the design and engineering phases of temporary and permanent works within major civil engineering projects.
Develop, review, and approve design calculations, drawings, and technical specifications.
Provide technical leadership on temporary works such as excavation systems, formwork, falsework, lifting operations, and working platforms.
Collaborate with project teams and subcontractors to ensure compliance with programme and safety requirements.
Offer guidance, mentorship, and support to junior engineers.
Conduct site visits and assessments to verify design assumptions and resolve technical issues.
Take part in coordination meetings, risk assessments, and constructability reviews.
Ensure adherence to CDM regulations, industry standards, and company design procedures.
Contribute to innovation and cost-effective design solutions that enhance safety and buildability.




Must-have Requirements

Degree in Civil or Structural Engineering (Master’s desirable).
Chartered Engineer (CEng) or actively working towards chartership.
Proven experience in temporary works design within civil engineering or construction.
Sound knowledge of British Standards and industry guidance for temporary works.
Competence in software such as AutoCAD, Revit, Tekla Tedds, STAAD Pro or similar.
Strong understanding of construction sequencing and methodologies.
Excellent leadership and stakeholder management skills.




Nice-to-have Skills

Experience with major infrastructure projects.
Continuous improvement mindset with exposure to innovative design techniques.




Apply Now

If you’re ready to take on this exciting challenge and lead temporary works design for a respected name in the UK construction sector, we’d love to hear from you. Apply today and take the next step in your engineering career.
