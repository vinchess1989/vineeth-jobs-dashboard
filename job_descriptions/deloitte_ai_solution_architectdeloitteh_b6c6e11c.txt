Company Description

Do you enjoy architecting how AI integrate into different solutions and processes? Do you enjoy engaging with clients to get a deep understanding of their goals and needs? Do you want to support the realization of the AI architecture and related benefits together with your colleagues and clients?

We are looking to strengthen our growing AI & Data team with AI Solution Architect, someone interested and experienced in planning and executing implementation of scalable AI solutions with our teams in our client projects.

About us

Deloitte's AI and Data services uncover relationships between business insights and organisational intelligence to unlock the power of existing data and drive progress for our client organisations. We do this by leveraging the power of analytics, automation and artificial intelligence (AI) to generate the insights that accelerate growth and efficiencies. This helps improve decision-making, increase operational efficiency, mitigate risk and more.

Job Description

Your role

As an AI Solution Architect in our AI & Data Team, being a part of our Technology & Transformation business unit, you will be responsible for:

Technical Vision & Architecture

Design and steer client engagements where organisations want to build scalable AI capabilities, covering both the AI solution architecture and how it fits into the overall enterprise landscape and business processes
Advise clients on how AI should be embedded into their existing architectures: not just as a "nice AI layer on top", considering integration, data, security, governance and "human in the loop" aspects so that solutions are robust, usable and sustainable

Execution Leadership

Shape and lead AI engagements: Define scope and approach, coordinate stakeholders and team members, and ensure the work stays on track while collaborating hands-on with technical teams on AI architectures and solutions (e.g. cloud-native and agentic AI patterns on Azure/AWS/GCP), bringing enough technical depth to challenge and guide design choices
Partner with client and Deloitte Solution Architect's and cross-functional teams for successful and scalable delivery
Provide technical oversight across project workstream; identify and resolve architectural dependencies and delivery risks proactively

Platform & Innovation

Evaluate emerging AI/ML, agentic frameworks, and automation technologies and make recommendations for adoption
Drive development and operationalization of AI-powered POCs towards scalable production solutions
Collaborate with our Engineering, AI & Data delivery methodology team on best ways to adopt valuable emerging concepts into delivery

Team & Stakeholder Engagement

Facilitate workshops and design sessions with business and IT stakeholders, translating needs into AI/architecture concepts and clear next steps
Build and nurture long-term relationships with stakeholders and clients by collaborating closely to understand their business needs and ensuring all engagements meet the highest quality standards
Support proposal development, including responding to RFPs and contributing to business development activities related to potential projects and engagements
Mentor and develop engineers, consultants, and architects working in the domain.
Act as a trusted advisor in client discussions on AI Solutions, striking the right balance between technical depth and business relevance, helping clients make realistic, value-driven decisions

You won't be working alone: You will collaborate with our global and local teams of diverse professionals, who bring a balanced mix of technical, legal, and business expertise to support you.

Qualifications

What we are looking for:

Technical skills:

5+ years of data/analytics/AI technology delivery experience, including at least 3 years in a senior architecture or technical leadership role, preferably in consultative capacity
Demonstrated expertise in designing and deploying agentic AI, LLM-driven automation, and multi-agent systems in enterprise environments. You have to not only know your way around the topic, but understand the implications agentic AI brings to planning and managing the architecture
Proficiency in Python, AI/ML model development, and workflow orchestration
Experience with cloud platforms (Azure, AWS, or GCP) for deploying AI and automation workloads
Strong knowledge of integration architecture, APIs, microservices, and CI/CD practices with solid understanding of enterprise architecture
Proven skillset through relevant (AI) architecture certifications from e.g. Azure, AWS or GCP
Familiarity with RAG architectures, vector databases, and agentic frameworks

Soft skills:

Ability to make complex analysis and communicate it in a concise and clear manner, appropriately tailored to people from a very wide range of cultures, technical backgrounds and seniorities
Strong organizational and time management skills, with the ability to priorities tasks effectively. Proven ability to execute and deliver, not just design or theorize
Ability to work independently and as part of a team with good coaching skills to support the professional development of junior colleagues
Strong analytical and problem-solving skills
Continuous learning mindset and willingness to stay updated with industry trends
You get satisfaction from helping organizations whilst forming and maintaining long-term business partnerships/relationships requiring effective interpersonal skills

Preferred/beneficial:

Experience of AI solutioning integrated to ERP/CRM environments
Experience with data/analytics/AI platforms such as Databricks, Snowflake, Fabric, AWS Sagemaker
Experience in implementation of Agentic AI and RPA platforms

Requirements:

Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, Business, or related field (advanced degree preferred with studies demonstrating technological competence)
Excellent command of the English language, Finnish (or Swedish) language is seen as an asset
You should possess a valid residence and work permit, candidates requiring relocation not being our primary option for this role
