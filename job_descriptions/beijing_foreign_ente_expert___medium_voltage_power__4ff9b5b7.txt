On behalf of world famous ICT company-Huawei, we are looking for a Expert - Medium Voltage Power Conversion Systems.




Huawei Heisenberg Research Center (Munich) is responsible for advanced technology research, architectural development, design and strategic engineering of our products.




Huawei Ohm Research Center (Nuremberg) is responsible for Advanced Technology Research, Architecture Evolution Design and Strategic Technology Planning.




Huawei Digital Power is a business unit within Huawei that provides Enterprise & Industry customers, products and solutions like clean power generation, transportation electrification, site power facilities, data center facilities, and embedded power. Huawei Digital Power is committed to integrate Digital and Power Electronics technologies to provide customers with high-quality, energy-efficient, green, and low-carbon power electronics products, facilitating their business success




The Power Conversion Technology Laboratory within Huawei’s Ohms Research Center in Nuremberg is responsible for Advanced Digital Power Technology Research, Architecture Evolution Design and Strategic Technology Planning, and plays an important role in the Huawei Digital Power business in the design and development of power converters and control systems for applications such as PV Solar & Battery Energy Storage Field, Data Centers, Electric Vehicle, and Embedded Power Supply.




Join us as an:

Expert - Medium Voltage Power Conversion Systems (m/f/d)




To contribute to the development of our Huawei Power Conversion Technology Laboratory, which plays an important role in designs and develops power converters and control systems for applications such as Photo Voltaic (PV), Data Center and Critical Power (DCCP), Electric Vehicle (EV), Board Mounted Power Supply (BMP) power ranges from several Watts to Mega Watts, and the most challenging things are high efficiency high density, cost effective and low static power consumption.




Your mission

You will be the technical authority in formulating requirements for R&D activities for Medium Voltage Power Conversion Systems
You will lead and execute the research and development of novel next generation MV architectures and converter structures for high power Solar PV, BESS, Data Center and EV charging applications
You will take ownership of the design, subsequent verification and validation of MV converter concepts in the R&D laboratories
You will closely collaborate with external R&D Institutes and Universities in developing novel converter and control algorithms for MV power electronic applications relevant to Huawei Digital Power
You will generate novel ideas and protect them with IPR fillings
You will lead and mentor cross discipline R&D Engineers




Your areas of expertise:

You hold a PhD or Master's degree in the field of power electronics, electrical and electronic engineering or in a similar engineering discipline
You shall possess several years of proven experience in Medium Voltage, high power electronics and system experience in product design or prototype development in an industrial context
You have a deep expertise in MV power electronic converter topologies, modulation, control and algorithm development, more focusing on system and Megawatt level power hardware design
You have a good working knowledge of MV standards and practical insulation coordination experience is desirable
You have a proven experience with new technology development initiatives, innovation and technical leadership
High level of self-reliance and an autonomous target-oriented work style, “can do” attitude, eager to learn new things and ready to think outside the box
You may have occasional travel related to ongoing projects, seminars, conferences etc.
You are fluent in written and spoken English, willing to learn German or additional languages are a plus

Your rewards of working here

Our culture is characterized by innovative power and team spirit as well as the intensive exchange of knowledge and experience within our global network.
To keep your development ongoing, you will find a broad range of training opportunities. Many online and face-to-face training programs incl. language courses in German and Mandarin.
Our diverse and welcoming environment is shaped by different backgrounds and around 40 individual nationalities.
Self-responsible work in a competent, motivated and constantly growing team.
