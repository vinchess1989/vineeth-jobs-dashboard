What you will do

In this role, you will drive the development of advanced 2.5D and 3D packaging technologies from early research through platform deployment across imec organization. Acting as the primary representative of the expert community in these domains, you will align the needs of multiple business lines into a coherent and achievable technology roadmap, ensuring imec can successfully deliver on its medium- and long-term ambitions in advanced packaging.

This is a highly visible leadership position combining strategic vision, technical expertise, organizational influence, and cross-functional coordination.

As 2.5D & 3D Technologies Director, You Will


Gather and consolidate the technology needs of all business lines related to 2.5D and 3D integration and packaging.
Contribute actively to the technology and business strategy development of these business lines.
Define and maintain a roadmap that meets business objectives while balancing resource availability and organizational capabilities.
Drive roadmap execution by coordinating activities across the relevant technical groups and expertise centers.
Represent the organization’s 2.5D and 3D expertise in discussions with internal and external stakeholders and support the development of new business opportunities.
Initiate and shape projects that bridge strategic technology gaps and create synergies across programs.
Work closely with project leaders, managers, and experts to ensure successful delivery of roadmap milestones.
Identify organizational, technical, or resource gaps that may jeopardize roadmap execution and lead escalation activities when needed.
Coach, mentor, and motivate key experts across the organization, fostering collaboration and enabling successful project execution.


What we do for you

We offer you the opportunity to join one of the world’s premier research centers in nanotechnology at its headquarters in Leuven, Belgium. With your talent, passion and expertise, you’ll become part of a team that makes the impossible possible. Together, we shape the technology that will determine the society of tomorrow.

We are committed to being an inclusive employer and proud of our open, multicultural, and informal working environment with ample possibilities to take initiative and show responsibility. We commit to supporting and guiding you in this process; not only with words but also with tangible actions. Through imec.academy, 'our corporate university', we actively invest in your development to further your technical and personal growth.

We are aware that your valuable contribution makes imec a top player in its field. Your energy and commitment are therefore appreciated by means of a market appropriate salary with many fringe benefits.

Who you are


You hold at least 15 years of experience in semiconductor R&D, with significant expertise in advanced packaging, 2.5D integration, and/or 3D integration technologies.
You have a proven track record of leadership, including at least 5 years of experience leading large teams, complex programs, or major technology initiatives.
You are passionate about emerging technologies and motivated by the opportunity to shape their future.
You possess deep technical expertise in advanced packaging, 2.5D integration, and 3D integration technologies.
You are an inspiring people leader who enjoys developing talent and enabling teams to succeed.
You combine strategic thinking with strong execution skills and a results-oriented mindset.
You have excellent communication, stakeholder management, and organizational skills, allowing you to build alignment across diverse groups and interests.
You are living in Belgium or are willing to relocate (support provided).
