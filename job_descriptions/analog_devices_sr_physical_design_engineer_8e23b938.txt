About Analog Devices

Analog Devices, Inc. (NASDAQ: ADI) is a global semiconductor leader that bridges the physical and digital worlds to enable breakthroughs at the Intelligent Edge. ADI combines analog, digital, AI, and software technologies into solutions that combat climate change, reliably connect humans and the world, and help drive advancements in automation and robotics, mobility, healthcare, energy and data centers. With revenue of more than $11 billion in FY25, ADI ensures today's innovators stay Ahead of What's Possible. Learn more at www.analog.com and on LinkedIn and X.


Own and drive full-chip and block-level PV signoff activities.
Perform and debug:
Design Rule Check (DRC)
Layout Versus Schematic (LVS)
Parasitic Extraction (PEX)
PERC/ERC checks
Antenna verification
Density and DFM checks
Analyze and resolve complex PV violations while maintaining schedule commitments.
Review and implement foundry rule deck updates.
Work with layout and design teams to achieve clean signoff.
Develop methodologies and automation to improve PV productivity and turnaround time.
Support tapeout readiness reviews and signoff documentation.
Perform Electromigration (EM) and IR Drop analysis on digital, analog, and mixed-signal designs.
Define power integrity verification strategies for blocks and full-chip designs.
Analyze static and dynamic IR drop issues and recommend corrective actions.
Debug EM violations and optimize power grid structures.
Correlate simulation results with design requirements and reliability specifications.
Collaborate with circuit, package, and PD teams for power delivery optimization.
Track and close reliability signoff requirements before tapeout.
Develop scripts and utilities using Python, Tcl, Shell, or SKILL to automate PV and EMIR flows.
Develop dashboards and reporting mechanisms for signoff metrics.
Drive continuous improvements in PV and EMIR methodology.
Interface with:
Physical Design teams
Layout teams
Circuit designers
CAD/EDA teams
Foundry support teams
Participate in design reviews, signoff reviews, and tapeout planning meetings.
Mentor junior engineers and provide technical guidance.
