Company Description

Job Description

As Chief Hardware Officer, you will lead the end-to-end development of proprietary tactile sensing hardware for the next generation of physical AI. From taking working prototypes through to volume manufacturing, you will own the firmware, MCU bring-up, and reliability qualification, building the critical touch layer that currently limits robotic autonomy and machine learning interaction.

Location

San Francisco, USA

Why this role is remarkable


Receive a C-level mandate and cofounder-level equity to build the tactile sensing layer for the future of robotics and physical AI.
Join a well-funded, stealth-stage founding team with a working prototype and a clear technical moat in a high-growth sector.
Transition from a working laboratory prototype to a scalable, manufacturable product that solves a fundamental bottleneck in modern autonomous systems.


What You Will Do


Own the hardware lifecycle from prototype to a manufacturable, qualifiable product, including firmware development and MCU bring-up.
Develop calibration and reliability qualification processes to ensure high-resolution tactile sensing scales effectively for volume manufacturing.
Set the long-term hardware roadmap and lead the hiring and management of a world-class hardware engineering team.


The ideal candidate


Proven track record of shipping real sensing, embedded, or MEMS/semiconductor hardware to production at scale.
Extremely hands-on technical leader who writes firmware, designs systems, and can personally debug MCUs during long bring-up cycles.
Previous experience as a founder or early engineer at a hardware startup, with a strong sense of personal accountability for delivery dates.
