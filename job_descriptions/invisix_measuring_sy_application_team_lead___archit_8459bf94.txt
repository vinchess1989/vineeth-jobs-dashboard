Invisix Measuring Systems is a fast-growing technology company developing a breakthrough tool for reconstructing the detailed 3D geometry of the most advanced next-generation semiconductor devices. As Application Team Lead / Architect, you will own the full applications engineering function: working directly with tier-1 semiconductor manufacturers to understand their device architectures and process challenges, and leading your team to deliver results that directly shape investment decisions.




This is not a software applications role. This is semiconductor applications engineering at its most demanding.




You will work extremely closely with the CEO initially, who has deep technical roots in this domain and has been leading customer relationships. The goal is to extract that knowledge and gradually take over the primary technical customer-facing role. When demos succeed, you are one of the most visible people in the organization, working on problems that genuinely matter at the frontier of what is physically possible. Demo success is existential for the company, so you need to thrive under that level of pressure.




You will report to the Head of Development and Engineering, manage the application team, and work from the Eindhoven office.




Key responsibilities:

Own the full applications engineering workflow: from understanding the customer's device architecture and process context, to developing the technical approach, to delivering results that prove the tool works for their specific challenge
Lead and mentor the application team, providing hands-on technical guidance on the most demanding work while developing team capability
Work closely with the CEO on customer strategy, gradually taking over the primary customer-facing technical role
Develop and maintain relationships with tier-1 semiconductor manufacturers at the highest technical level
Collaborate with algorithm, software, and hardware teams to bring together a complete solution for each customer
Contribute to long-term technical strategy and product development direction
Travel to customer sites approximately quarterly, with locations including Korea, Taiwan, the US, and Japan




About you:

PhD in physics, electrical engineering, or a related field
Proven experience at tier-1 semiconductor manufacturers (Samsung, TSMC, Intel, Micron, SK Hynix) or as an application engineer for major metrology suppliers
Previous role as an application engineer, application architect, or similar: customer-facing technical work where you owned the problem end to end
Deep, hands-on knowledge of advanced device architectures (logic and memory); able to draw and explain front-end structures from memory in full detail
Strong understanding of semiconductor front-end processes and their key metrology challenges
Experience with optical CD metrology recipe setup and/or inverse problems an advantage
Excellent programming capability in Python, C++, or similar, with strong knowledge of algorithms and numerical methods
Ability to thrive under intense pressure with high customer expectations, and the mindset that delivering results is non-negotiable
Fluent English for customer communication at all levels, with Korean and/or Mandarin Chinese an advantage




Benefits:

Competitive salary package
Employee Share Ownership Plan (ESOP)
Learning and development budget to support your professional growth
Flexible working arrangements




Invisix Measuring Systems

Semiconductor manufacturers can't build what they can't measure. At Invisix, we're solving one of the most technically demanding problems in chip manufacturing: reconstructing the detailed 3D geometry of the most advanced next-generation devices.




Founded by two ASML veterans with deep roots in short wavelength metrology, we've spent years de-risking this technology. Now we're building the team to bring it to market. If you love to take ownership, collaborate closely with innovators who care deeply about their contributions, and want your work to matter at the frontier of what's physically possible, this is the place to be.
