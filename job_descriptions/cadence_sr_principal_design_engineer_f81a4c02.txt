Job Title: Senior Principal Digital Implementation Engineer

Location: Cork, Munich, Katowice, Edinburgh

Reports to: Design Engineering Director

The Role

We are seeking an exceptional Senior Principal Engineer to provide technical leadership on some of the industry's most challenging silicon development programs.

In this role, you will apply deep expertise in silicon implementation and advanced EDA methodologies to solve problems and deliver results. You will work directly with strategic customers and internal Cadence teams to develop, deploy, and demonstrate innovative methodologies for advanced-node designs.

This is a hands-on technical leadership role. The successful candidate will combine outstanding semiconductor engineering expertise with the ability to influence lead a team of implementation engineers and technically contribute to architecture, methodology, tool development, and customer strategy.

Key Responsibilities


Provide technical leadership for complex, high-visibility silicon programs, Synthesis/Netlist through physical implementation, signoff, and tapeout.
Develop and deploy advanced methodologies for achieving aggressive power, performance, and area (PPA) objectives.
Work closely within the PSG engineering team to understand design implementation challenges and translate them into practical solutions.
Collaborate directly with Cadence R&D to identify product gaps, influence tool development, and drive enhancements required for advanced silicon designs.
Drive technical innovation across physical design, timing, power, signal integrity, physical verification, and signoff with the PSG European team
Evaluate and introduce new Cadence technologies, methodologies, and IP into customer design flows.
Lead technical investigations involving difficult silicon failures, performance limitations, convergence challenges, or methodology issues.
Establish reusable methodologies and best practices that can be deployed across multiple customers and technology nodes.
Mentor and develop our implementation engineers, serving as a technical authority within the PSG EMEA (Europe) organisation.
Represent Cadence as a technical thought leader with strategic customers, partners, and the wider semiconductor ecosystem.
Present technical results and innovations to senior leadership, customers, industry forums, and engineering communities.
Assist in the preparation of pre-sales support information and effort estimation with the Design Engineering Director and Sales tea,=m


Technical Scope

Depending On Experience, The Role May Encompass Areas Including


Advanced-node digital implementation
RTL-to-GDSII flows
Physical design and optimisation
Timing closure and signoff
Power optimisation and analysis
Signal integrity and electromigration
SoC integration and hierarchical implementation within a layout team
IP integration and reuse
Process/design technology co-optimization (DTCO)
Advanced packaging
Automation and methodology development including AI acceleration.


Required Qualifications


Bachelor's or Master's degree in Electrical Engineering, Computer Engineering, or a related discipline.
15+ years of relevant semiconductor engineering experience, or equivalent demonstrated technical achievement.
Proven tape-out experience on advanced process nodes.
Experience working with leading-edge CPUs, GPUs, AI/ML accelerators, networking devices, or other high-performance SoCs.
Extensive experience developing and delivering complex semiconductor products.
Deep expertise in one or more areas of the semiconductor implementation flow, combined with broad understanding of the complete SoC development lifecycle.
Demonstrated experience with advanced process technologies and complex physical implementation challenges.
Proven ability to diagnose and solve problems that cross traditional functional boundaries.
Experience working directly with semiconductor customers, foundries, or technology partners.
Excellent technical communication and presentation skills.
Demonstrated ability to influence technical direction without relying solely on organisational authority.
Experience of Technical Team leadership essential
Excellent oral and written English essential
Self-motivated with excellent planning, interpersonal, and communication skills


What Success Looks Like


A successful Senior Principal Engineer will:
Solve the problems others cannot. Take ownership of technically ambiguous, high-impact problems and drive them to resolution.
Enable customer success. Become a trusted technical partner to leading semiconductor companies and help them achieve ambitious silicon objectives.
Create leverage. Turn solutions developed for individual programs into reusable methodologies, tools, and engineering practices.
Lead through expertise. Raise the technical capability of the wider engineering organisation through mentoring, collaboration, and technical leadership.


Why Join the PSG Team?


This is an opportunity to work at the intersection of advanced semiconductor technology, EDA, and real silicon.
You will work on problems that are often at the limits of what current design methodologies and technologies can achieve, collaborating with some of the world's leading semiconductor companies and technology partners.
Rather than being limited to a single product or stage of the design flow, you will have the opportunity to influence the entire path from technology and methodology through to working silicon.
If you are an engineer who wants to solve the industry's hardest silicon problems—and see those solutions become real products—this is the team for you.
