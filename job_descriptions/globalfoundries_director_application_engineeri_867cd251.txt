About GlobalFoundries

GlobalFoundries is a leading full-service semiconductor foundry providing a unique combination of design, development, and fabrication services to some of the world’s most inspired technology companies. With a global manufacturing footprint spanning three continents, GlobalFoundries makes possible the technologies and systems that transform industries and give customers the power to shape their markets. For more information, visit www.gf.com.

Summary Of Role

This leadership role offers the opportunity to shape the future of semiconductor innovation by leading GlobalFoundries' Field Application Engineering organization supporting some of the industry's most strategic customers. The successful candidate will build and develop a high-performing team of FAEs, driving technical engagement, customer success, and design win strategy across emerging markets including AI infrastructure, silicon photonics, advanced packaging, networking, mobile, and power technologies. Serving as a critical link between customers, engineering, and business teams, this leader will influence technology roadmaps, accelerate revenue growth, remove technical barriers, and develop trusted executive-level relationships. The ideal candidate combines deep semiconductor expertise with proven people leadership, strategic thinking, and a passion for mentoring talent, while translating complex technical solutions into business outcomes that create long-term customer and company success.

Essential Responsibilities

Help Shape the Future of Semiconductor Innovation

GlobalFoundries is looking for a proven Field Application Engineering leader to drive technical engagement and design win strategy across our most strategic U.S. accounts.

This role sits at the intersection of technology, business, and customer success. You will lead a team of Field Application Engineers responsible for helping customers solve complex challenges, accelerating design wins, shaping technology roadmaps, and converting opportunities into long-term revenue growth.

You will partner with some of the world's most innovative technology companies and work across AI infrastructure, mobile, networking, silicon photonics, advanced packaging, and power.

What You'll Do

Lead and Inspire


Build, develop, and lead a world-class team of Field Application Engineers supporting GF's largest strategic accounts.
Create a culture of accountability, technical excellence, collaboration, and customer obsession.
Coach and develop future technical leaders while raising the talent bar across the organization.


Drive Growth


Partner with Account Managers to identify, qualify, and advance new business opportunities.
Lead the technical strategy behind design wins and major customer engagements.
Influence customer roadmaps and align GF's differentiated solutions to future market opportunities.
Translate customer requirements into business opportunities that drive growth and market share.


Deliver Customer Success


Lead customer engagements from initial evaluation through tapeout, production ramp, and lifecycle support.
Anticipate and remove technical barriers that could impact customer success.
Drive disciplined opportunity management, forecasting, and design win execution.


Build Strategic Relationships


Develop trusted partnerships with engineering, architecture, product, operations, and executive leaders.
Serve as a senior technical ambassador for GlobalFoundries.
Represent GF at key customer engagements, industry events, and executive briefings.


What Makes You Successful


Think strategically and act with urgency.
Uncover opportunities others miss.
Build credibility across engineering and executive audiences.
Translate complex technologies into compelling business outcomes.
Lead through influence, collaboration, and accountability.
Develop people, raise performance expectations, and create winning teams.


Other Responsibilities


Perform all activities in a safe and responsible manner and support all Environmental, Health, Safety & Security requirements and programs.


Required Qualifications


Bachelor's degree in Electrical Engineering or related technical discipline. Advanced degree preferred.
15+ years of semiconductor industry experience.
Experience leading customer-facing technical organizations.
Deep understanding of semiconductor technologies, foundry ecosystems, design flows, and design-win processes.
Proven success supporting strategic accounts.
Exceptional communication, presentation, and executive engagement skills.


Preferred Experience


Silicon Photonics
Advanced Packaging
AI Infrastructure
RF and Connectivity
Power and Mixed Signal Technologies
Hyperscale and Cloud Customers


Why GlobalFoundries

At GlobalFoundries, you'll have the opportunity to work with the world's leading technology companies while helping shape the next generation of semiconductor innovation.
