Embedded Software and Electronic Design | Thames Valley, South West & Wales

SoC Architect




Remote | Circa £130,000+ | Permanent




I’m working with a highly innovative technology company looking to appoint an experienced SoC Architect to help define and deliver a new generation of complex silicon.

This is a senior technical role with ownership across system architecture, performance modelling, HW/SW partitioning and architectural trade-offs across power, performance, area and timing.




You’ll work closely with RTL, verification, firmware, compiler and physical design teams, helping guide the design from early architecture through to tape-out and bring-up.




You should have:

Ownership of at least one ASIC or SoC taken to tape-out
Strong SoC, ASIC or processor architecture experience
Hands-on performance modelling in Python, C++ or similar
Strong knowledge of memory architecture, interconnects and HW/SW interfaces
Experience working with industry-standard EDA tool flows




Experience with compute accelerators, DSP, AI hardware or other high-performance silicon would be highly relevant.




Please apply with an up-to-date CV for a confidential discussion.
