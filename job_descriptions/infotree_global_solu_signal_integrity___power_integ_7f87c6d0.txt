Seeking an experienced Signal Integrity and Power Integrity Engineer to own DDR PHY channel and PDN architecture, system-level SI/PI modeling, simulation, and post-silicon validation.




The role involves debugging SI/PI issues across silicon, package, and board while collaborating with design, firmware, and hardware teams.
Strong experience with S/Z-parameter analysis, channel and PDN modeling, Ansys/Cadence tools, Python/Matlab, and silicon-to-simulation correlation is preferred.




Education:

Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Computer or Electrical Engineering required.
