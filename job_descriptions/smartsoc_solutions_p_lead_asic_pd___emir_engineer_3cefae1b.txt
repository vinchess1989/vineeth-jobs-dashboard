🚀 We're Hiring | Lead ASIC PD / EMIR Engineer




Are you passionate about power integrity and sign-off for advanced digital SoCs? We're looking for an experienced PDN / EMIR Engineer to join our growing VLSI team.




Required Experience




8+ years of overall VLSI/Physical Design experience is preferred.
3+ years of hands-on experience in PDN and EMIR closure for digital ASIC designs.
Strong expertise with industry-standard EMIR analysis and sign-off methodologies.
Excellent debugging, analytical, and cross-functional collaboration skills.




Key Responsibilities




Own SoC-level IR Drop, Signal EM, and Power EM sign-off for complex digital ASIC designs.
Drive EMIR closure by identifying root causes of power integrity and electromigration violations.
Debug challenging EMIR issues and provide optimized, practical solutions to achieve sign-off.
Collaborate closely with the Physical Design (PD) team to drive efficient IR/EM closure across the chip.
Analyze power delivery networks (PDN) and recommend improvements for robust power integrity.
Ensure timely closure of EMIR sign-off while meeting performance, power, and reliability targets.
