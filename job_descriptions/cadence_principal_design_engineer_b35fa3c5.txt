Job Title: Principal Digital Design Engineer

Location: Munich, Cork, Katowice, Edinburgh

Reports to: Senior Principal Engineer

The Role

We are seeking an exceptional Principal Digital Design Engineer to join the Performance Solutions Group (PSG).

The candidate will contribute to the development of complex, high-performance semiconductor designs.

You will work as part of a multidisciplinary engineering team, developing and integrating RTL for advanced SoCs and silicon platforms. You will be involved throughout the front-end design process, from understanding architectural requirements and developing microarchitectures through RTL implementation, simulation, synthesis, and handoff to downstream teams.

This is a hands-on engineering role suited to someone who enjoys solving challenging technical problems, learning new technologies, and working across the boundaries between architecture, RTL, verification, and physical implementation.

Key Responsibilities


Design, implement, and integrate high-quality synthesizable RTL using SystemVerilog.
Translate architectural specifications into robust, efficient microarchitectures and RTL implementations.
Work with architects and senior engineers to understand functional and performance requirements.
Develop RTL that meets requirements for performance, power, area, and functionality.
Participate in RTL design reviews and contribute to design methodology and coding standards.
Develop and maintain unit-level and block-level test environments in collaboration with verification engineers.
Debug functional issues using simulation, waveform analysis, assertions, and other verification tools.
Work with verification teams to identify, reproduce, and resolve RTL design issues.
Collaborate with physical design engineers to understand and address timing, area, power, and implementation constraints.
Support synthesis, timing analysis, linting, CDC analysis, and other front-end quality checks.
Contribute to integration of internally developed and third-party IP.
Help investigate and resolve issues encountered during integration and downstream implementation.
Document design decisions, interfaces, microarchitecture, implementation details and reviews within the PSG Quality Management System (QMS)
Contribute to continuous improvement of RTL methodologies, automation, and development flows.
Work alongside senior engineers to develop solutions to challenging customer and internal silicon problems.


Technical Scope

Depending On Experience, The Role May Encompass Areas Including


SystemVerilog RTL
SoC and subsystem design
CPU, GPU, AI/ML, networking, or other high-performance compute architectures
Control logic and datapaths
Memory interfaces and controllers
High-speed interfaces
Clock and reset architectures
AMBA protocols such as AXI, AHB, and APB
IP integration
Low-power design techniques
Assertions and formal verification
RTL synthesis and optimization
Static analysis, lint, and CDC
Performance and power optimization
RTL-to-gates implementation flows


Required Qualifications


Bachelor's or Master's degree in Electrical Engineering, Computer Engineering, or a related discipline.
10+ years of relevant RTL/ASIC design experience.
Proficiency in Verilog or SystemVerilog.
Understanding of digital logic design and synchronous design principles.
Experience developing synthesizable RTL for ASIC or FPGA implementations.
Familiarity with simulation and RTL debugging.
Understanding of common SoC interfaces and protocols.
Basic understanding of synthesis, timing, and physical implementation considerations.
Ability to analyze complex technical problems and develop practical solutions.
Strong written and verbal communication skills.
Ability to work effectively within a multidisciplinary engineering team.
Ability to communicate effectively to customers


What Success Looks Like


Deliver robust RTL. Develop clean, maintainable, synthesizable RTL that meets functional and performance requirements.
Think beyond the RTL. Understand how design decisions affect verification, synthesis, timing, power, area, and ultimately silicon.
Solve problems independently. Take ownership of assigned blocks and issues while knowing when to seek guidance from more senior engineers.
Work collaboratively. Build strong working relationships with architects, verification, physical design, and other Silicon Engineering teams.
Learn quickly. Develop expertise in new architectures, technologies, methodologies, and Cadence tools as projects evolve.
Grow into technical leadership. Develop the depth and breadth needed to take ownership of increasingly complex blocks and eventually lead technical areas within the Silicon Engineering organisation.


Why Join the PSG Team?


This is an opportunity to work at the intersection of advanced semiconductor technology, EDA, and real silicon.
You will work on problems that are often at the limits of what current design methodologies and technologies can achieve, collaborating with some of the world's leading semiconductor companies and technology partners.
Rather than being limited to a single product or stage of the design flow, you will have the opportunity to influence the entire path from technology and methodology through to working silicon.
If you are an engineer who wants to solve the industry's hardest silicon problems—and see those solutions become real products—this is the team for you.
