Siemens EDA is a global technology leader in Electronic Design Automation software. Our software tools enable companies around the world to develop highly innovative electronic products faster and more cost-effectively. Our customers use our tools to push the boundaries of technology and physics to deliver better products in the increasingly complex world of chip, board, and system design.

We are seeking an experienced and highly motivated Senior Hardware Engineer to join our hardware development team. The successful candidate will be responsible for the design, development, verification, and delivery of complex high-speed digital hardware solutions. This role involves PCB design for FPGA-based systems, signal and power integrity considerations, manufacturing support, and collaboration with multidisciplinary teams throughout the product lifecycle.

Job Responsibilities


Design and develop complex FPGA-based, high-speed digital hardware and multilayer PCBs.
Implement and optimize interfaces such as DDR4, PCIe, Ethernet, USB, HDMI, and DisplayPort.
Perform hardware validation, debugging, and support manufacturing and product bring-up activities.
Collaborate with cross-functional teams to ensure reliable, high-quality product


Required Qualifications


Bachelor's or Master's degree in Electrical Engineering, Electronics Engineering, or a related field.
Experience in PCB and hardware design.
Strong experience designing FPGA-based hardware platforms.
Proven expertise in high-speed digital design with:
DDR4 memory interfaces
PCI Express (PCIe)
Hands-on experience with Siemens Xpedition design environment.
Strong knowledge of schematic capture, PCB layout, and hardware debugging.
Excellent written and verbal communication skills in English.

Preferred Qualifications


Experience with PCB Signal Integrity (SI) simulation using Siemens toolchain.
Experience with PCB Power Integrity (PI) simulation using Siemens toolchain.
Experience designing high layer-count PCBs.
Knowledge of high-speed communication interfaces:
Ethernet
USB
HDMI
DisplayPort (DP)
Experience with embedded software development and hardware bring-up.
Knowledge of HDL languages such as Verilog or VHDL.
Proficiency in scripting languages such as Python or TCL.
Experience defining manufacturing tests and working directly with contract manufacturers.
Strong understanding of EMC, EMI, ESD, and product safety requirements.
